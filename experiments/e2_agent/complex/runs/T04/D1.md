# T04 — Month-End Close (Multi-Currency) — Canonical Basis D1

Pass: 1 (reduction of search_1.md). No prior canonical decomposition.

---

## D — Subtasks

D-1 **Rate table preparation and lock** — Obtain exchange rates from an authoritative named source for the period; establish three rate types (spot/transaction, period-average, closing); lock the table; no updates permitted after lock.

D-2 **AR sub-ledger completeness and lock** — Post all invoices, credit notes, cash receipts, and write-offs with transaction date ≤ cutoff; lock the AR sub-ledger to the close period.

D-3 **AP sub-ledger completeness and lock** — Post all vendor invoices, debit memos, and payment runs with date ≤ cutoff; verify GR/IR account status; lock the AP sub-ledger.

D-4 **Inventory/cost sub-ledger completeness and lock** — Post all goods movements, cost adjustments, and valuation entries with date ≤ cutoff; lock the inventory sub-ledger.

D-5 **Fixed asset sub-ledger: depreciation, additions, disposals, lock** — Run depreciation; post additions, disposals, and impairments with date ≤ cutoff; confirm net book value per sub-ledger ties to GL fixed-asset control account; lock.

D-6 **Payroll sub-ledger posting and lock** — Confirm payroll journal (gross pay, employer taxes, net pay, deductions) fully posted to GL; verify payroll clearing account nets to zero; lock.

D-7 **Sub-ledger ↔ GL control account reconciliation** — For each sub-ledger, reconcile total balance to its GL control account at line-item level; explain and resolve all differences before proceeding to GL work.

D-8 **Accruals and deferrals posting** — Post standard recurring accruals, non-standard manual accruals (with documented approvals), GR/IR accruals, prepaid amortisation, interest accruals, and deferred revenue recognition; assign auto-reversal dates to first day of N+1.

D-9 **Bank reconciliation** — Obtain complete bank statements (all accounts, full calendar month through cutoff); match GL cash entries to bank lines; document outstanding checks, deposits in transit, and bank charges; produce signed reconciliation (adjusted bank balance = adjusted book balance) per account; perform in both transaction currency and functional currency for foreign-currency accounts.

D-10 **FX revaluation** — Identify all monetary GL accounts; apply closing rate at open-item level (AR/AP) and account-balance level (cash/loans); post unrealized FX gain/loss to designated accounts; route long-term IC monetary items (IAS 21 §32, repayment not planned) to OCI; generate per-item revaluation report.

D-11 **Intercompany balance confirmation and out-of-balance investigation** — Confirm IC pairs across all in-scope entities using IC reference or agreement number; identify and resolve (or formally explain) all out-of-balance amounts before elimination runs.

D-12 **Intercompany elimination** — Eliminate IC AR/AP, revenue/expense, loans/receivables/payables, and investment/equity per the IC entity matrix; allocate and eliminate IC FX differences; apply bottom-up elimination order for multi-level chains (lowest tier first); post all elimination journals to the consolidation ledger.

D-13 **Trial balance extraction** — Specify and document ledger set and period; extract TB with transaction-CCY, functional-CCY, and presentation-CCY columns; include all entities, segments, and accounts with activity or balance; verify zero-net in all currency columns.

D-14 **Trial balance review, sign-off, and hard close** — Perform analytic review of account movements against prior-period; investigate unusual items; obtain controller sign-off; lock period (Hard-Close state); log close event; stage auto-reversals for N+1 release when N+1 opens.

## Dep — Dependency seams

Dep-1 **Rate table lock → FX revaluation (D-10)** — Rate table must be locked before D-10 runs. *Breaks if absent: two runs of revaluation use different rate tables; results non-reproducible; wrong closing rate applied to all monetary balances.*

Dep-2 **Rate table lock → IC elimination (D-12)** — The same locked rate table used for revaluation must govern the IC FX difference calculation in D-12. *Breaks if absent: rate updated between revaluation and IC elimination; IC FX difference is non-zero and cannot be explained.*

Dep-3 **All sub-ledger batches + accruals + payroll + depreciation complete in GL → FX revaluation (D-10)** — D-2 through D-8 (all postings) must be in GL before D-10 runs. *Breaks if absent: late postings (depreciation, payroll, accruals) are not covered by revaluation; monetary exposure mis-stated for those amounts.*

Dep-4 **Sub-ledger ↔ GL reconciliation clean (D-7) → FX revaluation (D-10)** — D-7 differences must be resolved before D-10. *Breaks if absent: revaluation applies closing rate to inflated or deflated starting balance; FX gain/loss overstated or understated.*

Dep-5 **Foreign-CCY accruals classified as monetary → FX revaluation filter (D-10)** — Accruals posted in foreign currencies must be present in GL and classified as monetary items before D-10 runs. *Breaks if absent: foreign-currency accrual excluded from revaluation filter; functional-currency exposure appears in P&L but not in the FX gain/loss line.*

Dep-6 **AR/AP cash entries posted to GL → bank reconciliation (D-9)** — Last-day payment runs and receipts must be in GL before D-9 begins. *Breaks if absent: recon shows false uncleared items that self-resolve, masking genuinely outstanding items.*

Dep-7 **Sub-ledger lock (D-2/D-3) precedes accrual GR/IR scan (D-8)** — AP sub-ledger must be locked before D-8's GR/IR scan reads the snapshot. *Breaks if absent: AP invoice and GR/IR accrual both post to the same period; payables double-counted.*

Dep-8 **FX revaluation (D-10) → IC elimination (D-12)** — D-10 must complete before D-12; IC balances used for elimination must be post-revaluation balances at closing rate. *Breaks if absent: elimination on pre-revaluation balances; post-revaluation IC balances do not zero out; unexplained residual appears in OCI.*

Dep-9 **IC balance confirmation complete (D-11) → IC elimination (D-12)** — D-11 out-of-balance investigation must be resolved before D-12 runs. *Breaks if absent: elimination on unconfirmed balances; residual errors attributed to FX differences, masking the real mismatch.*

Dep-10 **Consolidation scope file current → IC elimination (D-12)** — Scope file must reflect all entity additions and disposals in the period before D-12 runs. *Breaks if absent: entity disposed of mid-period still in scope; its IC transactions eliminated when it is no longer a subsidiary; minority interest not adjusted.*

Dep-11 **IC elimination journals in GL (D-12) → trial balance extraction (D-13)** — D-12 must be complete before D-13. *Breaks if absent: partially eliminated or unconsolidated view circulated as the consolidated TB.*

Dep-12 **FX revaluation journals in GL (D-10) → trial balance extraction (D-13)** — D-10 posting date must fall within the close period before D-13 runs. *Breaks if absent: FX P&L line absent from period-N TB (e.g., revaluation job uses system run date = day 1 of N+1).*

Dep-13 **Bank reconciliation sign-off (D-9) → TB sign-off (D-14)** — D-9 must be signed off before D-14 TB sign-off. *Breaks if absent: TB signed off with unverified cash balance.*

Dep-14 **Hard close (D-14) → auto-reversal release to N+1** — Reversals are staged at D-14 hard close and released only when N+1 opens; N+1 must not be opened prematurely to accommodate a reversal. *Breaks if absent: reversal lands in period N, netting out the accrual before TB is extracted; or N+1 contaminated by premature opening.*

## V — Criteria

*Each item is a decidable predicate over the produced result.*

### Structural invariants

V-1 **Double-entry integrity** — Every journal entry in every sub-ledger and the GL has debits = credits in both transaction currency and functional currency; enforced at system level. *Falsifier: sub-ledger allows single-sided entry under "auto-offset" logic; GL control account becomes wrong.*

V-2 **Single-period tagging** — Every journal line carries exactly one period; no single entry spans two periods; cross-period allocations use two opposite entries in separate periods. *Falsifier: amortisation spreads a single entry across two periods; neither period is correct.*

V-3 **Functional-currency amount frozen at posting** — Functional-currency equivalent is stored at posting time using the then-applicable rate; never re-derived retroactively when rate tables change. *Falsifier: rate table update retroactively changes functional amounts; prior TB restated without notice.*

V-4 **Complete audit trail** — Every state change to every journal (creation, approval, modification, reversal, deletion) recorded with actor, timestamp, and reason; deletions are logical only (no physical deletes). *Falsifier: entry physically deleted; original cannot be reconstructed.*

V-5 **IC symmetry before elimination** — At close, before D-12, every IC transaction in entity A has a corresponding entry in entity B; difference above materiality threshold triggers formal investigation, not silent carry-forward. *Falsifier: $500 IC asymmetry carried to suspense every month; cumulative balance never investigated.*

V-6 **Single locked rate table for entire close** — One rate table, locked before D-10, governs all currency-dependent steps (revaluation, translation, IC FX difference); no partial updates mid-close. *Falsifier: rate updated between revaluation and IC elimination; eliminated balance differs from revalued balance.*

V-7 **Accrual/actual type segregation** — Accrual journals carry a distinct journal type from actual invoices; the distinction is queryable at journal-type level. *Falsifier: payroll accrual posted with type "invoice"; accrual reversal creates apparent duplicate payment.*

V-8 **Hard close is final; post-close modifications are controlled and logged** — After hard close, the ledger is read-only; any entry in a locked period requires formal authorization (CFO or above), generates an exception report, and flags the TB version as restated; hard-close event is logged (initiator, timestamp, period, overrides); any re-open produces an auditable record with sign-off. *Falsifier: system allows a "quiet edit"; or period re-opened and re-closed with no record of interim state.*

### System configuration criteria

V-9 **Posting controls system-enforced** — Period status transitions (Open → Soft-Close → Hard-Close → Archived) are system-enforced; posting to a locked period is rejected by the system, not by manual review; transitions are irreversible without a documented override. *Falsifier: status is a flag with no enforcement; postings continue to a "closed" period.*

V-10 **Prior-period data locked in archive** — Prior-period TB is read-only once archived; comparative columns pull from archive, not live ledger; restatements of prior period are explicitly flagged. *Falsifier: prior-period restatement corrupts comparative column without disclosure.*

V-11 **Monetary/non-monetary account classification** — COA explicitly marks each account as monetary (revalue at closing rate) or non-monetary (historical rate); revaluation routine picks up only monetary accounts. *Falsifier: non-monetary account (prepaid expense) revalued; spurious FX gain/loss.*

V-12 **Single functional currency per entity** — Each legal entity has exactly one designated functional currency; all balances ultimately expressed in it. *Falsifier: entity posts without a functional currency setting; balances untranslatable.*

V-13 **Three-amount journal line** — Every journal line carries transaction-CCY amount, functional-CCY amount, and presentation-CCY amount; none may be null. *Falsifier: sub-ledger stores only transaction amount; GL receives no functional-currency amount; revaluation has no base.*

V-14 **Translation distinct from revaluation** — Translation (functional → presentation) is a separate computational step from revaluation (remeasurement of monetary items at closing rate); gain/loss from each step posts to separate designated accounts. *Falsifier: revaluation gain/loss and translation difference commingled in the same P&L line.*

V-15 **Long-term IC monetary items route to OCI** — FX differences on IC monetary items where repayment is not planned or likely (IAS 21 §32) are posted to OCI, not P&L; these items are explicitly tagged per agreement. *Falsifier: IC loan tagged as short-term; FX swings hit group P&L volatility incorrectly.*

V-16 **IC entity matrix complete and current** — Register of all IC pairs, the accounts used for IC receivables/payables/revenue/expense, and the elimination accounts is maintained; additions/disposals in the period reflected before D-12 runs. *Falsifier: new IC relationship not in matrix; transactions escape elimination.*

V-17 **Ledger scope defined** — Statistical/memo ledgers are separate from the primary ledger; consolidation-adjustment ledger is separate from both; TB is drawn from a documented ledger set that excludes statistical. *Falsifier: memo entries inflate reported balances; or consolidation-adjustment ledger accidentally excluded, so IC eliminations missing from TB.*

V-18 **Rate table provenance and immutability** — Rate table records the authoritative source (named feed), rate effective date per currency, extraction timestamp, and approver sign-off; rates are immutable after lock. *Falsifier: auditor cannot verify which rate was used because table was overwritten; or stale prior-period rate applied due to data-feed gap.*

V-19 **Sub-ledger–GL mapping version-controlled** — Sub-ledger-to-GL account mapping table is version-controlled; tested after any COA change; a broken mapping causes posting to a suspense account (detectable). *Falsifier: COA account renamed; mapping breaks silently; sub-ledger posts to suspense.*

### Per-component output criteria

V-20 **Sub-ledger ↔ GL reconciliation at line-item level, retained** — Reconciliation performed at individual-line level (not only total); result stored as a versioned artefact per close period. *Falsifier: sub-ledger and GL agree in total via batch netting; individual-line offsets invisible; or reconciliation run but not saved.*

V-21 **Bank reconciliation complete, signed, and retained** — Each bank account has a signed reconciliation (adjusted bank balance = adjusted book balance); reconciling items are aged; items above threshold investigated; reconciliation stored with preparer name, reviewer name, date, and bank statement version. *Falsifier: recon "completed" but not signed; or aged uncleared items rolled forward without investigation; or prior-period evidence overwritten.*

V-22 **Multi-currency bank recon in both currencies** — Foreign-currency bank accounts are reconciled in transaction currency AND functional currency; the functional-currency difference is explicitly identified as a revaluation input. *Falsifier: recon done in transaction currency only; functional-currency cash balance unchecked.*

V-23 **Open-item-level revaluation with correct partial-settlement splitting** — AR and AP revalued at individual open-item level; partial payment realises gain/loss on the settled portion only; remaining open item continues to be revalued. *Falsifier: AR revalued as account total; settlement produces wrong realised gain; or partial payment treated as full settlement and remaining item no longer revalued.*

V-24 **Revaluation posting date = close period** — Revaluation journals carry the close-period posting date, not the system run date; running the job on day 1 of N+1 must not post to N+1. *Falsifier: DST or timezone bug causes posting date = N+1; FX P&L absent from period-N TB.*

V-25 **Revaluation idempotent** — A second revaluation run in the same period reverses and replaces the first; net revaluation balance is unchanged after re-run. *Falsifier: two runs produce double the gain/loss.*

V-26 **Revaluation run report generated and retained** — Report lists each revalued account/open item, rate applied, prior balance, revalued balance, and gain/loss; stored per run as audit evidence. *Falsifier: revaluation runs silently; no per-item trace available to auditor.*

V-27 **Foreign-CCY accruals classified as monetary in revaluation filter** — All accrual accounts denominated in foreign currency are classified as monetary items and included in the revaluation filter. *Falsifier: EUR accrual on USD-functional entity excluded from revaluation filter; exposure appears in P&L but not in the FX gain/loss line.*

V-28 **IC matching uses entity matrix, not bare account code** — IC transactions matched by IC reference or agreement number via the IC entity matrix; account-code suffix variants handled through the matrix; post-elimination IC difference report shows zero residual in all IC accounts (or residuals documented). *Falsifier: matching uses exact account code; accounts 1300 and 1300A treated as different; pair unmatched.*

V-29 **IC investment/equity elimination bottom-up** — In multi-level chains, elimination applied bottom-up (lowest tier first); parent investment eliminated against post-elimination subsidiary equity. *Falsifier: A-B eliminated before B-C; B's equity still contains C's un-eliminated profit; A's elimination against wrong equity figure.*

V-30 **Intragroup unrealised profit eliminated** — Unrealised profit on inventory or fixed assets transferred between group entities is eliminated; only profit realised through external sale is recognised. *Falsifier: subsidiary sells inventory to parent at markup; markup in consolidated profit even though goods unsold externally.*

V-31 **IC dividend: internal leg only eliminated** — Intragroup declared dividend: IC receivable/payable eliminated; WHT payable to an external tax authority is not eliminated. *Falsifier: entire IC dividend entry eliminated including external WHT payable; tax liability disappears from consolidated BS.*

V-32 **Accrual cutoff rule documented and consistently applied** — Expenses (revenue) recognised in the period the obligation (right) arises, regardless of invoice/cash date; standard recurring accrual schedule reviewed each period for continued applicability. *Falsifier: utility invoice for N consumption booked in N+1; or cancelled contract still accrued because schedule not updated.*

V-33 **Non-standard accruals documented with rationale and approval** — One-off estimates include documented business rationale, quantification methodology, and approver sign-off in journal header or attached workpaper. *Falsifier: large provision booked as "month-end provision" with no quantification; auditor cannot validate.*

V-34 **GR/IR clearing account at zero** — GR/IR clearing account nets to zero after close (or contains only legitimate timing items with explanation); GR/IR accrual does not overlap AP sub-ledger amounts for the same goods receipt. *Falsifier: GR/IR not cleared; payables accrual overlaps AP sub-ledger; payables double-counted.*

V-35 **Accrual completeness verified beyond prior-month repeat** — A scan of open POs, long-term contracts, and prior-month accrual schedule confirms no new obligation is omitted; reliance solely on prior-period roll-forward is not acceptable. *Falsifier: new obligation missed because process only repeats last month's accruals.*

V-36 **Auto-reversal posts to same accounts as original** — Each auto-reversal journal posts to the same accounts as the original accrual; confirmed by comparing reversal template to original entry in the auto-reversal log. *Falsifier: original entry corrected but reversal template not updated; reversal hits a different account.*

V-37 **Trial balance zero-net in all currency columns** — Sum of debit balances = sum of credit balances applied to raw (un-netted) amounts; verified independently in each currency column (transaction, functional, presentation). *Falsifier: extraction script nets before summing; wrong-sign entry passes undetected; or functional-currency column does not net to zero, indicating a revaluation posting error.*

V-38 **TB entity and account completeness** — TB includes every legal entity, every segment/cost centre, and every account with any activity or balance; zero-balance accounts on a statutory disclosure list are not suppressed. *Falsifier: dormant entity with residual IC balance excluded; or zero-balance share-capital line suppressed, blocking statutory note.*

V-39 **TB version-stamped and immutable** — Each extraction stamped with extraction timestamp, ledger version, and rate table version; final signed-off version is immutable; subsequent extractions create a new version number. *Falsifier: corrected TB overwrites original; auditor cannot see what changed; multiple versions in circulation undifferentiated.*

### Auditability criteria

V-40 **Journal unique ID and source lineage** — Every journal line carries a unique, non-reusable ID; reversals reference the original entry ID; source document reference present (invoice #, bank ref, etc.). *Falsifier: two journals share same ID due to batch import conflict; individual entries untraceable.*

V-41 **Dual authorisation on material journals** — Journals above materiality threshold require a second approver (preparer ≠ approver); approval timestamp recorded. *Falsifier: large manual accrual approved by preparer alone; segregation-of-duties violation.*

V-42 **IC elimination workpaper retained** — Workpaper shows each matched IC pair, elimination journal, and any residual with explanation; stored per close period. *Falsifier: elimination "completed" but no record of which pairs matched; scope creep or omissions undetectable.*

V-43 **Consolidation scope file archived per period** — The legal entity register used for each close (in-scope, out-of-scope, additions, disposals) is archived before the next period's file is updated. *Falsifier: scope file updated for next month before current month's evidence is archived; scope history lost.*

### Edge/boundary criteria

V-44 **Date and time zone definitions unambiguous** — Closing rate, bank statement cutoff, and posting cutoff explicitly specify last business day vs. last calendar day; cutoff rule references a single canonical time zone for all entities. *Falsifier: closing rate from Dec 30 (Friday) for a Dec 31 close, one day stale; or IC transaction booked December by Tokyo, January by London, creating unmatched IC pair.*

V-45 **Functional currency change mid-period handled correctly** — Change requires restatement at the date of change; prior-period comparative retains original functional currency; change is disclosed. *Falsifier: functional currency changed retroactively; prior-period revaluation gains/losses restated without disclosure.*

V-46 **New GL account mid-period reflected in all tables** — COA mapping, revaluation account rules, and IC elimination account tables updated for any new account before close runs. *Falsifier: new IC account not in elimination table; transactions to that account escape elimination.*

V-47 **Entity disposed mid-period consolidated pro-rata** — Subsidiary sold intra-period consolidated only up to disposal date; IC eliminations apply only to that sub-period; gain/loss on disposal recognised in group P&L. *Falsifier: subsidiary included for full period; excess profit and IC balances not prorated.*

V-48 **Hyperinflationary entity treated separately (IAS 29)** — Non-monetary items of a hyperinflationary-economy entity are restated to current purchasing power before translation; standard FX revaluation does not apply to that entity. *Falsifier: hyperinflationary entity processed through standard revaluation; balance sheet understates real values.*

V-49 **Dual-book entity: only one set feeds consolidation** — Entity maintaining two sets of books feeds only one into the consolidated TB; mappings are separate and non-overlapping. *Falsifier: IFRS and local-GAAP depreciation both flow into consolidation; asset balance double-counted.*

V-50 **Three-currency IC transactions require three rate pairs** — IC transaction where the transaction currency matches neither entity's functional currency: each entity translates to its functional currency; consolidation translates to presentation; three rate pairs explicitly applied. *Falsifier: system assumes IC transaction currency = one entity's functional currency; EUR transaction mismapped.*

V-51 **Suspense accounts net to zero at hard close** — Any non-zero suspense balance at hard close requires documented resolution; IC FX differences routed to suspense must be allocated before period lock. *Falsifier: IC FX difference routed to suspense each month; cumulative balance never resolved.*

V-52 **Depreciation run covers all asset classes** — Depreciation run exception report reviewed; newly added asset classes producing zero depreciation confirmed intentional or corrected before FA lock. *Falsifier: new asset class added with no matching depreciation rule; assets produce zero depreciation silently.*

V-53 **Negative inventory valued at cost** — Inventory GL account balance reflects actual cost for all positions including negative quantities; negative inventory not suppressed to zero. *Falsifier: negative inventory valued at zero; cost of sales understated.*

V-54 **TB analytic review before sign-off** — Sign-off includes analytic comparison of account movements against prior-period; unusual movements investigated and explained; zero-net check alone is not sufficient for sign-off. *Falsifier: TB signed without analytic review; material accrual omission undetected.*

## N — Scope exclusions

N-1 **Tax provision (current and deferred computation)** — Tax calculation is a downstream process fed by the pre-tax TB; out of scope. *Exception: IAS 12 deferred tax journal entries booked as part of close are in scope as an accrual-type step.*

N-2 **Payroll gross-to-net computation** — Payroll processing (gross-to-net, withholding calculations) occurs in a separate system; only the resulting payroll journal entry, its posting, and its reconciliation are in scope.

N-3 **Management reporting cost-centre allocations** — Inter-cost-centre allocations for management P&L are a post-close analytics step and do not affect the statutory TB; out of scope. *Exception: recharges generating real IC invoices between legal entities are IC transactions in scope.*

N-4 **Budget vs. actual variance reporting** — Analytics layer downstream of TB; does not affect close correctness; out of scope. *Exception: if budget data lives in the same ledger, the extraction logic that excludes it is in scope.*

N-5 **Cash flow statement preparation** — Derived from TB and disclosure notes; out of scope. *Exception: non-cash item tagging required for the indirect method must be correct in GL as part of close.*

N-6 **External audit execution** — Auditors' own procedures are out of scope; the close must produce auditable records (V-4, V-18, V-20, V-26, V-39–V-43), but designing audit procedures is not.

N-7 **Statutory consolidation above immediate group** — Ultimate-parent goodwill allocation, full minority interest computation beyond what is required for IC elimination, and legal-entity statutory filings are out of scope. *Exception: if the consolidated TB produced is the final statutory product, goodwill and full minority interest computations become in scope.*

N-8 **Transfer pricing compliance** — Whether IC prices are arm's-length is a tax/TP matter; the close assumes IC prices are set and eliminates them. *Exception: TP adjustment journals booked in the close period are in scope as a specific journal type.*
