# D3 — Canonical Basis: Subscription Billing Logic (T01)

Pass 3 reduction: D2 + search_3 holes folded in. Full re-emit of the canonical decomposition.

---

## D — Subtasks / Components

**D1. Plan & period model** — Represent each plan as (amount, currency, billing_period ∈ {monthly, annual}); compute billing periods as half-open intervals [start, end) anchored to a declared timezone; derive next-period boundary as prev.end = next.start with no gap and no overlap. *Falsifier: DST/timezone drift inserts a free day or double-bills a day; gap or overlap between periods bills the customer incorrectly.*

**D2. Proration calculator** — Compute proration_factor = days_remaining / days_in_current_period (billing-timezone denominator supplied by D1); apply to credit (old_plan_price) and charge (new_plan_price); for annual↔monthly direction, use price-per-day = plan_price / days_in_period, not the complementary plan's price. For the n-th plan change in one period, old_plan_price must equal the price of the (n−1)-th plan, not the original plan's price. *Falsifier: constant denominator fails on Feb, 31-day months, leap years; wrong rate direction systematically mis-credits the customer; n-th change computed against the original plan price ignores the intermediate plan and produces a wrong credit in both directions.*

**D3. Invoice assembly & lifecycle** — Build invoice as Σ(line_items) + tax − applied_credits; each line item must be attributed to exactly one billing period. Maintain an explicit invoice state model: draft → open → finalized → voided | written-off; declare all valid state transitions. Voided means the invoice never represented a collectible debt and emits no ledger entry; written-off means the debt existed but is declared uncollectable and must emit a bad-debt ledger entry. Seal as an immutable document on finalization. At a scheduled plan-change firing at the renewal boundary, exactly one invoice topology must be declared: (a) a single invoice for the new period under the new plan, or (b) a closing invoice for the terminal period of the old plan plus a separate opening invoice for the new period; the chosen topology determines whether the outstanding-invoice resolution rule (Dep-4) applies to renewal-boundary plan changes or only to mid-period changes. *Falsifier: orphaned line item causes revenue leakage with no audit trail; mutation after finalization breaks the audit trail; written-off invoice treated as voided suppresses the bad-debt ledger entry; voided invoice not marked terminal is retried by dunning; undeclared topology at renewal boundary blocks a routine scheduled downgrade (topology b) or allows concurrent open invoices on a past_due renewal+downgrade (topology a).*

**D4. Tax computation** — Compute tax_amount = taxable_base × rate(country, plan_type, effective_date) from a versioned rate table; determine taxable_base per country as either tax-inclusive (back-compute net) or tax-exclusive (gross-up). *Falsifier: unversioned rate table applies a new rate to already-finalized invoices; inclusive/exclusive mismatch inflates stated price and reports wrong net revenue.*

**D5. Credit ledger** — Maintain running account_balance = Σcredit_ledger_entries − Σdebit_ledger_entries; write a ledger row on every credit or debit event; apply credits before the charge attempt; enforce balance ≥ 0. *Falsifier: balance updated without a ledger row creates a phantom balance that reconciliation cannot explain; negative balance passes undetected until the gateway rejects a negative-amount charge.*

**D6. Charge & dunning engine** — Issue every charge attempt with an idempotency key; build dunning schedule as an ordered list of retry intervals anchored to the charge-failure timestamp; model grace period as an explicit subscription state (see D8); transition subscription to a declared terminal state after max retries. *Falsifier: missing idempotency key causes double-charge on network retry; schedule not anchored to failure time allows indefinitely-timed or post-cancellation retries.*

**D7. Refund processor** — Compute full_refund = total net paid on invoice (gross charges − prior partial refunds on same invoice); partial_refund = explicitly specified amount ≤ net paid (never re-derived from current plan price); issue every refund attempt under a unique idempotency key; issue a credit note for every refund; select refund destination (original payment method vs. account credit) explicitly. *Falsifier: second full refund on a partially-refunded invoice over-refunds; re-deriving refund amount after a plan change gives wrong value; absent destination selection fails silently on an expired payment method; missing idempotency key on refund allows a timed-out-and-retried request to produce two refund records each individually passing the net-paid check.*

**D8. Subscription lifecycle FSM** — States: {trialing, active, grace_period, past_due, paused, canceled}; all transitions must be declared and exhaustive. grace_period is the state entered immediately after a missed payment; dunning retries do not begin until the subscription exits grace_period to past_due after the grace window elapses. The following policies must each be explicit:
- trial_end anchored to trial_start timestamp (not signup); exactly one trial-converted event at trial end.
- plan change while trialing: exactly one declared policy — (a) immediate active conversion, (b) staged for trial_end, or (c) new plan with original trial_end preserved; the chosen path must not emit a second trial-converted event on an already-active subscription.
- trial end with no payment method on file: a distinct FSM transition must be declared (e.g., to a "payment_method_required" hold state, to past_due, or to immediate cancellation); this path is separate from Dep-16 (gateway unavailable) and must be explicit (see Dep-32).
- pause entry = immediate or at period boundary; pause exit triggers billing for remaining days; period extension from pause is additive across multiple pause/resume cycles within the same billing period; when pause overlaps an active dunning window, the extension covers only days the customer genuinely did not receive service, excluding dunning-collection days (see Dep-31).
- max pause duration: exactly one declared transition when the maximum is exceeded — auto-cancel, forced resume (triggering a charge for the remaining period), or a declared hold state; the subscription must not remain in paused indefinitely with no billing and no cancellation path.
- cancellation effective date = immediate or end-of-period; reactivation anchor = reactivation_date.
- reactivation billing: exactly one declared rule for what is charged on reactivation — a full new period from reactivation_date, or a proration to the next natural renewal boundary; the rule must be stated so that both immediate and end-of-period cancellations produce a deterministic first charge (see Dep-29).
- plan-change effective date = immediate (proration applies) or next renewal (no proration); any pending scheduled plan change must be declared disposed — canceled, held pending reactivation, or promoted to immediate — on every lifecycle transition that precedes the scheduled renewal (pause, past_due, cancellation); a new scheduled-change request arriving while a prior scheduled change is already pending must either replace the prior request with an audit record or be rejected — silent overwrite and dual-fire at renewal are both invalid.

*Falsifier: implicit or missing transition leaves status inconsistent with billing events; wrong anchor date shifts billing; plan change during trial silently converts to active without a declared policy; scheduled downgrade fires after reactivation against a new billing period; grace_period conflated with past_due causes dunning to fire before the grace window elapses; max pause exceeded leaves subscription paused indefinitely; second scheduled change silently overwrites the first or both fire at renewal producing two consecutive plan-change events.*

## Dep — Cross-Component Dependency Seams

**Dep-1. D2 + D4 → D3 (plan-change × proration × tax)** — Each proration line (credit and charge) carries its own independent tax computation; tax must NOT be computed on the net proration amount. *Breaks: tax on the net amount gives wrong result in jurisdictions with minimum thresholds or tiered rates.*

**Dep-2. D8 → D5 (trial-end × credit balance)** — At trial conversion, any existing account credit must be consumed before the first charge attempt is issued. *Breaks: first charge ignores existing credits; customer charged more than owed.*

**Dep-3. D8 → D2 (trial-end × annual plan)** — The trial-conversion event triggers a full annual charge; no day-offset from the trial period is applied to the annual billing amount. *Breaks: trial-day proration applied to the annual price produces a partial-year first charge.*

**Dep-4. D6 + D8 → D3 (dunning × plan-change [past_due + upgrade])** — An outstanding unpaid invoice must be resolved (paid, voided, or merged) before or alongside any new plan charge when the subscription is in past_due. *Breaks: two concurrent invoices leave dunning with an ambiguous collection target.*

**Dep-5. D6 → D8 (dunning × cancellation)** — Cancellation during active dunning must declare whether the unpaid invoice is voided or remains collectible. *Breaks: implicit void loses revenue; implicit keep blocks reactivation by requiring payment of an aged invoice.*

**Dep-6. D8 + D2 → D7 (cancellation × annual proration)** — Immediate cancellation of an annual plan must apply a declared, consistent policy on whether the remaining period generates a refund. *Breaks: undeclared policy produces different outcomes depending on which code path is reached.*

**Dep-7. D8 → D5 (cancellation × credits)** — Unused account credits at cancellation must be assigned one declared fate: refunded to customer, forfeited, or held for reactivation. *Breaks: no policy causes credits to silently vanish or block account closure.*

**Dep-8. D8 + D6 → D3 (reactivation × dunning debt)** — On reactivation, any unpaid invoice from the prior dunning cycle must be explicitly handled (revived, written off, or merged) before a new invoice is created. *Breaks: new invoice created while old debt is silently written off or double-counted.*

**Dep-9. D8 + D2 → D6 (pause × billing period [resume charge])** — On resume from a mid-period pause, the charge covers only the days remaining at pause, not the full period. *Breaks: full-period charge on resume double-bills the pre-pause days.*

**Dep-10. D8 → D1 (pause × annual renewal date [single cycle])** — A single paused interval must shift the annual renewal date by the exact pause duration. *Breaks: renewal fires on the original anniversary, cutting the customer's paid term short.*

**Dep-11. D6 → D8 (pause × dunning [past_due gate])** — A subscription in past_due must not enter pause until payment is resolved, or pausing must continue dunning retries without interruption. *Breaks: pausing a past_due subscription halts retries; unpaid invoice ages silently with no collection action.*

**Dep-12. D7 → D4 (refund × tax)** — A refund on a taxed invoice must include the proportional tax portion, not only the pre-tax amount. *Breaks: partial refund of the pre-tax amount leaves the customer under-refunded by the tax fraction.*

**Dep-13. D7 → D5 (refund → credit balance)** — A refund issued as account credit must write a ledger entry; all subsequent invoices must consume the resulting balance. *Breaks: credit created by the refund not reflected in the ledger causes future charges to ignore it.*

**Dep-14. D5 → D4 (credit balance × tax)** — Where jurisdiction rules require it, if a credit reduces the invoice amount the taxable base must be recomputed on the reduced amount before tax is finalized. *Breaks: tax computed on gross then credit applied — customer pays tax on an amount they were credited back.*

**Dep-15. D5 + D6 → D8 (annual × monthly co-existence [dunning scope])** — Account-level credits apply across all subscriptions; dunning on one subscription must not trigger cancellation of other, healthy subscriptions on the same account. *Breaks: dunning logic scoped to the account cancels a healthy subscription alongside the delinquent one.*

**Dep-16. D6 → D8 (trial-end × gateway-down)** — If the payment gateway is unavailable at trial end, the subscription must be routed into dunning retry logic, not transitioned to canceled. *Breaks: gateway-down at trial end marks the subscription past_due or canceled instead of queuing a retry.*

**Dep-17. D8 → D4 (reactivation × country change)** — Tax country is re-evaluated at reactivation date, not inherited from the canceled subscription record. *Breaks: old tax country applied to the first post-reactivation invoice produces wrong tax.*

**Dep-18. D1 → D2 (proration × leap year)** — D2 must use the actual period length (days_in_current_period) supplied by D1, not a constant 365. *Breaks: annual period containing Feb 29 has 366 days; using 365 is an off-by-one error.*

**Dep-19. D5 → D7 (credit application × partial refund)** — The refundable base on an invoice is invoice_total − applied_credits (net amount charged), not the gross invoice total. *Breaks: refund against gross total refunds more than the customer actually paid.*

**Dep-20. D5 → D2 (multiple plan changes in one period)** — Credits from the first plan change must be reflected in the credit balance before computing the second proration charge. *Breaks: each change computed against the original plan price; accumulated credits ignored; customer over-charged.*

**Dep-21. D6 → D8 (charge in flight × plan change)** — A plan change must not be finalized until any pending charge attempt on the current subscription has settled (success or failure). *Breaks: plan recorded as upgraded before charge result arrives; if charge fails, plan is upgraded with no payment.*

**Dep-22. D2 → D5 (proration credit disposition — ledger vs. line item)** — The proration credit computed by D2 must be placed by exactly one mechanism: either as a negative line item directly on the new invoice (immediate offset), or as a ledger entry in D5 for application to the invoice. Exactly one path must be chosen and consistently applied. *Breaks: credit written to the ledger AND placed as a line item on the same invoice double-credits the customer; credit written only to the ledger but not applied to the current invoice leaves the customer charged the full new-plan price this period.*

**Dep-23. D5 × D6 (credit debit idempotency across dunning retries)** — The credit debit applied against an invoice must be bound to the charge's idempotency key: if a charge attempt fails and is retried, the credit debit must not be re-applied. *Breaks: first charge attempt debits credits then fails; retry re-debits the same credits; ledger shows a double debit; account balance goes negative; V3 may pass at the point of the first debit but fails on the second.*

**Dep-24. D4 → D3 (mid-active-period billing-country change)** — When a customer changes billing country while the subscription is active, exactly one policy must be declared and enforced: (a) new rate applies from the next invoice only (open invoice unaffected), or (b) open invoice tax is recalculated before finalization. Policy (b) is only valid before finalization; after finalization V1 prohibits mutation. *Breaks: undeclared policy allows an implementer to choose either path; identical inputs produce different tax amounts depending on which path is reached; policy (b) applied post-finalization retroactively mutates a sealed invoice.*

**Dep-25. D8 → D1 (multiple pause/resume cycles × additive period extension)** — When two or more pause/resume cycles occur within the same billing period, the renewal date must be extended by the sum of all pause durations, not only the most recent one. *Breaks: second resume resets the extension to only the second pause duration; the customer loses credit for the first pause interval and is billed for days not received.*

**Dep-26. D3 → D6 (zero-amount invoice non-initiation)** — Invoice finalization must gate the charge initiation path: if amount_due = 0, D6 must not issue a charge attempt. The check must occur as an explicit handoff condition, not solely as a post-hoc invariant. *Breaks: D6 receives the finalized invoice and skips the amount_due check; a $0 authorization is submitted to the gateway; some gateways log $0 charges as valid transactions, creating a charge record with no corresponding receivable.*

**Dep-27. D7 × D5 (refund attempt idempotency × ledger atomicity)** — The V8 net-paid check and the corresponding ledger debit in D5 must be evaluated atomically under a refund idempotency key. A concurrent or retried refund request must not pass the V8 check before a prior request's ledger debit is committed. *Breaks: two concurrent refund requests both pass V8 individually before either commits; together they exceed net paid; the over-refund is not caught by billing logic and is only visible at reconciliation.*

**Dep-28. D3 → D5 (invoice write-off × bad-debt ledger entry)** — Transitioning an invoice to written-off state in D3 must atomically trigger a bad-debt ledger entry in D5; a void transition must not. *Breaks: written-off invoice treated as a void emits no ledger entry; reconciliation cannot distinguish debt losses from billing errors; the credit ledger balance is overstated.*

**Dep-29. D8 + D2 → D1 (annual→monthly downgrade × new monthly period anchor)** — When an annual plan is downgraded to monthly, exactly one rule must declare when the first monthly billing period starts: from the change date, from the original annual anniversary, or from the next natural calendar-month boundary. The anchor determines all subsequent monthly renewal dates. *Breaks: monthly billing anchored to the original annual anniversary fires the next charge nine months in the future instead of one month; anchor set to change date but second period computed from anniversary fires before the first month elapses; absent rule makes the monthly billing schedule implementation-dependent.*

**Dep-30. D3 + D8 → D6 (renewal-boundary plan-change invoice topology × Dep-4 applicability)** — The invoice topology declared in D3 for a scheduled plan change firing at renewal determines whether Dep-4's outstanding-invoice resolution requirement applies: under topology (b) (closing + opening invoice), Dep-4 must be enforced at renewal just as for mid-period changes; under topology (a) (single new-period invoice), Dep-4 does not apply at the renewal boundary because no prior-period invoice exists. The applicability rule must be declared to match the chosen topology. *Breaks: topology (b) chosen but Dep-4 not enforced at renewal allows a past_due renewal+downgrade to produce two concurrent open invoices; topology (a) chosen but Dep-4 enforced at renewal blocks a routine scheduled downgrade that has no outstanding prior invoice.*

**Dep-31. D8 + D6 → D1 (pause extension during dunning × service-days exclusion)** — When Dep-11's path is taken (subscription paused while dunning retries continue), the additive pause extension that shifts the renewal date (Dep-10, Dep-25) must cover only days the customer genuinely did not receive service; days during which a dunning retry was being processed against the subscription must not count toward the pause extension. *Breaks: subscription is past_due and paused; dunning succeeds on day N of the pause; resume extends the renewal date by N days; customer gains N paid days they were already being collected against during the dunning window.*

**Dep-32. D8 → D6 (trial end × no payment method on file)** — When trial_end fires and no payment method is on file (gateway available but nothing to charge), the FSM must follow a declared, distinct transition: e.g., to a hold state pending payment method addition, to past_due with immediate dunning, or to immediate cancellation. This path is separate from Dep-16 (gateway unavailable) and requires its own explicit policy. *Breaks: trial ends with no payment method; system transitions to canceled, losing the customer without opportunity to add a method; or transitions to active with no charge attempt queued and no dunning, leaving the subscription permanently unpaid.*

## V — Criteria

*(Each is a decidable predicate over the produced result.)*

**V1. Invoice total invariant + immutability** — invoice_total = Σ(line_items) + tax − applied_credits at finalization; the document is immutable thereafter — no retroactive mutation of any component, including tax recalculation triggered by a mid-period country change.

**V2. Charge idempotency** — Processing the same charge event twice produces exactly one charge record; no duplicate revenue entry and no double-charge to the customer.

**V3. Ledger balance consistency** — account_balance = Σcredit_ledger_entries − Σdebit_ledger_entries at all times; every credit or debit event has a corresponding ledger row.

**V4. No overlapping active periods** — No two billing periods for the same subscription overlap in time.

**V5. Currency consistency** — All line items, tax, and credits on one invoice share the same currency.

**V6. Tax-period consistency** — The tax rate applied to an invoice is the rate effective at invoice issue date, not at period start or payment date.

**V7. Charge attempt ↔ invoice linkage** — Every charge attempt is traceable to exactly one invoice; no orphaned charge attempt exists without a corresponding invoice.

**V8. Refund ≤ net paid** — Total refunds on an invoice cannot exceed total net amount collected (gross charges minus all prior partial refunds on the same invoice).

**V9. Active subscription ↔ open period** — Every subscription in {trialing, active, past_due} has exactly one open billing period; none has zero or more than one.

**V10. Zero-amount invoice** — When applied credits fully cover the invoice, amount_due = 0 and no charge attempt is issued.

**V11. Negative invoice** — When credits exceed the invoice total, the excess is added to account balance and no outbound payment is triggered.

**V12. Same-day plan change** — A plan change on day 1 of the billing period produces: proration credit = 0, new-plan charge = full period (no division-by-zero, no 100% credit on the old plan).

**V13. Zero-day trial** — A zero-length trial produces the same result as an immediate paid subscription; no trial-converted event is emitted.

**V14. Cancellation during trial** — No invoice, no line items, and no refund attempt are present in the output when a subscription is canceled while still in trial.

**V15. Pause on day 1 of period** — After a pause begun on day 1 of a period and subsequent resume, the billing output shows the full period provided with no charge for the paused interval.

**V16. Resume on boundary day of max pause window** — A resume event on the last day of the allowed pause duration is treated as a valid resume; no auto-cancellation is triggered.

**V17. Same-day cancellation and reactivation** — When cancellation and reactivation timestamps fall on the same calendar day, their ordering is determined by timestamp; the subscription is not left in an inconsistent state.

**V18. Feb 29 annual anniversary** — An annual renewal date of Feb 29 is rolled to Feb 28 in the following non-leap year; no date-arithmetic exception occurs.

**V19. Dunning terminal state** — After all dunning retries are exhausted, the subscription is in a declared terminal state; it is not left active with no collection path and dunning does not run indefinitely.

**V20. No charge to a canceled subscription** — No charge record is created for a subscription whose status is canceled, even if a dunning retry job was enqueued before the cancellation.

**V21. Tax completeness at finalization** — Every finalized invoice in a taxable jurisdiction carries a non-null, correctly computed tax_amount; no invoice is finalized while its tax computation is still pending.

**V22. No refund against a pending charge** — No refund record is created against a charge attempt that has not yet reached a terminal gateway state (success or failure).

**V23. Credits not consumed by canceled subscription** — No credit balance decrement is attributed to a subscription with status = canceled; the balance remains available for active subscriptions only.

**V24. Annual→monthly downgrade credit rate** — The proration credit for an annual→monthly downgrade equals annual_price × (days_remaining / days_in_year), not unused_full_months × monthly_price.

**V25. Monthly→annual upgrade invoice completeness** — A monthly→annual upgrade invoice contains both the full annual charge line and the remaining monthly-days credit line; neither is absent.

**V26. Monetary rounding invariant** — A single rounding strategy (rounding mode and per-line vs. per-invoice application) must be declared and applied consistently to all monetary division operations (proration_factor × price, tax_rate × base); invoice_total = Σ(rounded line_items) + rounded_tax − applied_credits must hold exactly, not just approximately. *Falsifier: per-line rounding on a multi-line invoice accumulates penny errors; V1 fails by $0.01; a partial refund individually satisfies V8 but over-refunds by the rounding residual.*

**V27. Same-plan change is a no-op** — A plan-change request where new_plan_id = current_plan_id must be rejected or treated as a no-op: no invoice is created, no ledger entry is written, and no charge attempt is issued. *Falsifier: the plan-change code path executes and D2 computes equal and opposite proration lines; D3 creates a $0 invoice; D6 may still issue a $0 charge attempt (violating V10) and the spurious invoice complicates the audit trail.*

**V28. Tax on a zero-amount invoice** — When applied credits fully reduce amount_due to $0, the system must declare and consistently apply exactly one of: (a) tax = $0 (no taxable supply, credit instrument fully settles the obligation) or (b) tax = computed amount with a separate tax credit note. The declared policy must hold uniformly across all jurisdictions for which it is selected. *Falsifier: reporting tax = $0 in a jurisdiction that treats a credit instrument as a taxable payment means a taxable supply went unreported; reporting non-zero tax without a corresponding credit note leaves the customer holding a tax liability on an invoice they paid nothing for.*

## N — Declared Scope Exclusions

**N1. Payment gateway communication** — Sending charge requests to the gateway is infrastructure; this scope receives only the outcome (success / failed / pending) as input. *In only if retry timing must be governed by specific gateway response codes — the dunning schedule's input signal already models this boundary.*

**N2. Email / notification dispatch** — Sending dunning emails and billing notices is a side effect of state transitions, not calculation logic. *In only if email-send failure is required to gate a billing state transition.*

**N3. Payment method storage / tokenization** — The card-token vault is infrastructure; calculation logic does not touch it. *In only if payment method type affects tax treatment (e.g., B2B purchase order vs. consumer card).*

**N4. Accounting / GL export** — Posting to the general ledger is downstream of invoice creation; GL consumes finalized invoices. *In only if revenue-recognition rules (ASC 606) determine when a charge line is booked, affecting invoice timing.*

**N5. Product catalog / feature entitlement** — What features a plan grants is out of scope; calculation needs only plan price and billing period. *In only if entitlements affect proration (e.g., seat-based or usage-based billing).*

**N6. Fraud detection** — Detecting abnormal charge patterns is a separate system; billing logic does not gate on fraud scores. *In only if a fraud-flagged charge must be voided before invoice finalization.*

**N7. Multi-currency FX rates** — Out if all plans are denominated in a single currency. *In as soon as a credit earned in one currency must be applied to an invoice in another.*

**N8. Coupon / discount codes** — Discounts and coupon codes that modify the billed amount are not listed in the task's capability set and are out of scope. *In only if a discount affects the proration base (e.g., a discount is applied before proration_factor is computed) — at that point the discount interacts with D2 and D4 in a non-trivial way and must be modeled explicitly.*
