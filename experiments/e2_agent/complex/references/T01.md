## Task `T` (verbatim from `tasks/T01.md` / PLAN §3 T01)

> Design the subscription billing logic — the rules of what to charge a customer and when (not the
> infrastructure and not the project, specifically the calculation logic) — that stays correct under any
> combination of the following capabilities co-existing in one product:
> - monthly and annual plans;
> - mid-period plan change (upgrade/downgrade) with proration;
> - trial period converting to paid;
> - refunds (full and partial);
> - account credits / balance;
> - country-dependent tax;
> - failed charges and retries (dunning);
> - cancellation and reactivation;
> - pause and resume.
> Decompose this task.

**Shape.** Pairwise-dense: a set of billing capabilities that genuinely **couple on a single money event**.
The load-bearing content is NOT the component list (billing-with-proration is the single most-blogged
decomposition example on the web — its parts are widely recalled). It is **(i) the global money/ledger
invariants** that span every capability and **(ii) the NEGLECTED policy boundaries** (rounding, the tax
engine, fraud) whose silent omission turns an external decision into a billing bug. T01 is the dataset's
**CALIBRATION / null-anchor**: a saturable, famous task whose seam count is *not* a density discriminator —
but its reference must still be a genuine, complete, minimal GFSO basis. Achievement goal: a correct
rule-set that *terminates* in a specified set of rules, not a maintenance regime.

**Defect-class (distinctness from T07, the other pairwise-dense task).** T07's coupling is
concurrency/consistency over shared mutable state (interleaving-sensitive, race-bearing). T01's coupling is
**money-conservation over a sequential state-machine** — value-conserving and deterministic-in-order: the
hard part is that several capabilities (proration, tax, credit, refund) all touch the *same money event* and
must **net to a single conserved total**, and that lifecycle transitions (pause/cancel/reactivate, trial→paid)
cross proration and dunning boundaries. A forgotten seam here is a real billing bug that *leaks or
double-counts money*, not a race.

**Single meta-level.** This decomposes the **billing logic** at one level: the capabilities and the rules
coupling them on a money event. It does NOT spawn sub-projects (integrate the payment gateway, build the tax
service, run a finance close, staff a billing team). Components are rules of one design, not deliverables
owned by different functions. (Consequence: the OPERATIONAL failure modes {FM-5,6,7} — runtime/execution-time
modes — are mostly out of scope for this static single-meta-level decomposition, EXCEPT where the billing
task's own retry/dunning/idempotency machinery forces a design-time operational predicate; see §7.)
**Authority/control is ORTHOGONAL** (who may issue a refund / approve a credit is a delegation-plane
predicate, not a billing-correctness node — see §3).

**Scope anchor (the `spec`).** The goal `spec` is "billing logic correct under **any co-existence** of the
nine capabilities." Its two halves are: (a) **each capability is present as a separable rule-set** (the `D`
passes) and **each cross-capability money-event coupling is right** (the `Dep`-seams); and (b) **the produced
rule-set is money-correct** (the spanning invariant criteria — money conserved, invoices reconcile, tax on
the right base/time, ledger append-only, no over-refund, entitlement coupled, exactly-once per period). A
decomposition is complete iff it covers both halves.

## 1. Subtasks `D` — the billing capabilities / passes (the parts)

> `D` = the children of the decomposition (canon §2.2: `D: T → P(T)`, a DAG).

Each subtask is a separable **rule-set** with its own pass/fail criterion on its *output* (the
amount/state it produces), not a self-report ("proration implemented"). The **invoice/charge computation**
that turns line items into a final amount is treated as ONE subtask (the assembly point every other
capability feeds), not smeared across all of them — its *interactions* with each capability are the `Dep`-seams.

| # | Name | What it requires | Truth-maker (covered iff the decomposition names…) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **D1** | **Subscription lifecycle / state machine** | The states a subscription occupies (trialing, active, past_due/dunning, paused, canceled, expired) and the legal transitions between them (trial→active, active→paused→active, active→canceled→reactivated, active→past_due→active/canceled). Each transition defines whether and what it bills. This is the spine the money events hang on. | …a subscription state machine / lifecycle with named states and transitions that drive billing. Credit "subscription has states (trial, active, paused, canceled, past_due)" and "transitions trigger or stop billing." |
| **D2** | **Plan & pricing model** | The catalog of plans and how a price is determined: monthly vs annual billing period, the per-plan amount, currency, and the period→amount mapping. Defines what a full-period charge is *before* any proration/tax/credit. | …a plan/pricing model with billing period (monthly/annual) and per-plan price. Credit "plans have a price and a period (monthly/annual)." The base amount everything else adjusts. |
| **D3** | **Billing-cycle / schedule engine (anchor & period boundaries)** | The rule that decides *when* a charge is due: the billing anchor date, period start/end, next-renewal computation, and how the period boundary is defined (the instant proration is measured against). Credit "prorate when a plan changes mid-cycle." The capability the plan-change and lifecycle seams (Dep3, Dep8) rest on. |

Handles tax-exempt customers (V-E4) and the externally-supplied rate/jurisdiction (N2). | …a tax-computation step keyed on the customer's country/jurisdiction applied to the charge, **with tax-inclusive vs tax-exclusive pricing defined** (inclusive backs the tax out of the stated price — charging base+tax on an inclusive plan over-charges). Credit "compute tax by country," "add VAT/sales tax," "price-includes-tax vs tax-on-top," "stacked tax components as separate lines." The mechanism the tax-base seams (Dep1, Dep2) and V-I4 rest on. |
| **D7** | **Payment / charge execution & dunning (retry)** | Submit the computed amount to the payment method, record the result (success/decline), and on failure run the dunning policy: schedule retries, move the subscription to past_due, and define the give-up/cancel terminal. | **D8** | **Account credit / balance ledger** | The customer's stored credit/balance: how credit is *created* (proration credit, refund-to-credit, goodwill, overpayment) and *consumed* (applied to reduce a future invoice total — Dep4). Credit "customer balance / account credits applied to charges." The mechanism Dep4 (credit→total) and the conservation invariants (V-I1, V-I5) rest on. |
| **D9** | **Refund (full & partial)** | Reverse money already captured: a full or partial refund against a prior charge, bounded by what was actually captured (V-I6), routed either back to the payment method or to account credit (Dep5). Defines what a refund reverses (including its tax portion — Dep6). | …a refund capability (full and partial) that reverses a prior charge to the payment method or to credit. Credit "issue full/partial refunds." The capability Dep5, Dep6, and V-I6 rest on. |

> **Note on D4 vs the others.** Invoice/charge computation (D4) is a *distinct subtask*, not a property
> folded into each capability. It is listed once; its *interactions* — proration feeds it (Dep3 via the
> proration-tax order Dep1), credit reduces its total (Dep4), tax is computed on its net base (Dep1/Dep2) —
> are the `Dep`-seams. An agent who says only "sum the line items" has named D4 but not the *order/base* seams
> (Dep1, Dep2, Dep4) that make the sum correct. **Trial** is part of D1 (a lifecycle state with a zero/deferred
> charge, see V-E5), not a separate subtask; **cancellation/reactivation** and **pause/resume** are transitions
> of D1 (their *billing* consequences are the Dep8 seam), not standalone subtasks — folding them keeps the
> design at one meta-level.

## 2. Dependencies `Dep` — cross-capability couplings as explicit, separately-falsifiable `Dep` edges

> `Dep` = the canon primitive (§2.2): an acyclic relation on `T × T`; `(tₐ, tᵦ) ∈ Dep` iff `criteria(tᵦ)`
> references the output of `tₐ` (with coherence `deadline(tₐ) < deadline(tᵦ)`). **Each `Dep` edge is a
> criterion of a child that references a sibling's output — the glue** — and carries its **own** falsifying
> truth-maker (the anti-mock condition), not "these capabilities relate" prose.

A **real seam** is an edge where the output of one capability feeds the input of another **on the same money
event**, and the edge carries its **own** falsifying criterion — a concrete scenario in which money is
created, lost, double-counted, or computed on the wrong base if the edge is mishandled (Constitution Ст. II.5
anti-mock). Not "these capabilities relate" prose. A seam is covered iff the decomposition names the **same
capability pair** and the **same thing that breaks**. **T01 is the CALIBRATION task — the real seam set is
~8–12, at the top of the C(9,2)=36 pairwise upper bound** (PLAN §3 T01); most capability pairs (e.g.
annual-plan × tax-jurisdiction, trial × pause) share no truth-maker and are **nominal, not real, seams** —
correctly absent. **One-defect-one-place:** each `Dep` is a coupling with its own breakage; a defect-coupling
criterion (§4.3) is kept only if it has NO `Dep`-twin (see §4.3).

| # | Name | Pair (capability → capability) | What the edge requires | Truth-maker (covered iff named with its breakage) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Dep1** | **Tax on the prorated amount (tax base = net, not gross)** | D5 proration → D6 tax | Tax must be computed on the **prorated** amount, not the full-period price. The proration output is the taxable base; computing tax on the gross plan price over-charges tax on a mid-cycle change. The shared artifact is the *net taxable amount* on the invoice. | …that tax is applied to the **prorated** (net) amount, not the full plan price — tax base = the proration result. Shared artifact = the taxable base. **The single most-forgotten T01 seam:** proration and tax are designed separately and tax silently runs on gross. |
| **Dep2** | **Tax base after credit application (and the credit-vs-tax ordering)** | D8 credit → D6 tax → D4 total | Whether account credit reduces the **taxable base** or only the **post-tax payable** must be a defined order, and applied consistently. The wrong order computes tax on an amount the customer never effectively pays (or under-taxes). The shared artifact is *what the tax line is computed on* once credit is in play. | …that the order of credit-application vs tax is defined (credit reduces taxable base **or** post-tax total, stated, not accidental) so tax isn't computed on the wrong base. Shared artifact = the tax base in the presence of credit. **Distinct from Dep1** (Dep1 = proration→tax base; Dep2 = credit→tax base/order). |
| **Dep3** | **Mid-cycle plan change = credit old + charge new at correct rates** | D5 proration → D2 pricing → D4 total | An upgrade/downgrade mid-period must **credit the unused portion of the old plan at the old rate** AND **charge the used/remaining portion of the new plan at the new rate**, netted on one invoice — not charge the full new plan, not forget the old-plan credit. Shared artifact = the proration credit+debit pair. **A defining money-conservation seam** — drop the old-plan credit and the customer is over-charged. |
| **Dep4** | **Credit application reduces the invoice total** | D8 credit → D4 total | Available account credit must be applied to **reduce the amount charged** to the payment method, drawing down the balance by exactly the applied amount (no credit created or lost — V-I1/V-I5). > **Anti-mock note.** Every `Dep` references a concrete *shared artifact on a money event* (the taxable base,
> the net total, the credit line ↔ balance entry, the refund disbursement split by tender, the proration
> credit+debit pair, the currency/rounding convention, the period anchor, the conversion charge, the credit
> draw-down ↔ its compensating restore) — a forgotten seam here is an objectively identifiable **billing bug**
> that leaks/double-counts money or taxes the wrong base, not a nominal pair. None collapse onto each other:
> different capability pair OR different falsifier ⇒ distinct seam (canon §2.2 non-redundancy). In particular
> Dep1 (tax-on-prorated-base) ≠ Dep2 (tax-base-after-credit/order) ≠ Dep7 (rounding/currency consistency) —
> three different things tax/total can get wrong; Dep3 (plan-change proration) ≠ Dep8 (lifecycle-transition
> proration/anchor); Dep5 (refund destination/tender-split) ≠ Dep6 (refund tax portion); Dep4 (credit apply on
> success) ≠ Dep10 (credit roll-back on card-fail).
> **The anti-mock filter excludes nominal pairs** that share no truth-maker: annual-plan × tax-jurisdiction
> (a yearly period doesn't couple with which country's rate applies — each is independent), trial × pause (a
> trial isn't paused-and-resumed as a billing period), monthly-vs-annual × refund-routing (the period length
> doesn't change where a refund goes) — these are NOT real seams and are correctly absent. **The real seam set
> is 10, at the top of the PLAN's ~8–12 calibration band, well below the 36 pairwise pairs.**

> **FM mapping for `Dep`.** A forgotten/violated `Dep` edge is an **FM-1** defect (a spanning integration
> criterion — the glue — not written / not covered; §4.2 FM-1 insufficiency, §18.10 *hole-i*) when it is a
> coverage hole, and an **FM-2** defect (criteria of two children incompatible / a relational defect at fixed
> composition; §4.2 FM-2) for the two-sided / shared-artifact seams whose breakage is a mutual-satisfiability
> conflict (Dep2 credit-vs-tax order, Dep4 single-apply credit↔balance, Dep5 mixed-tender split, Dep7 one
> currency/rounding regime, Dep10 credit draw-down ↔ compensating restore). The §7 FM table records this. Per
> one-defect-one-place, a seam's failure is scored **here as a `Dep` item**, never re-listed as a separate
> criterion.

## 3. Authority `Del` — orthogonal note (NOT scored)

> **`Del` is the canon "who" axis** (§2.3: `Del: T → A`, exactly one agent per task) — **orthogonal** to the
> decomposition plane `{D, Dep, V}` and **NOT scored** as a decomposition item. This is a plane-separation
> note, NOT a NEGLECTED exclusion, NOT a criterion, NOT a completeness hole.

Who is *authorized* to issue a refund, approve a credit, override a price, or grant an exemption — and any
segregation-of-duties / maker-checker over billing operations — is **not** a predicate over the produced
artifact (a correct, money-conserving rule-set) and is **not** a decomposition node. A billing engine can be
perfectly money-conserving, reconciling, and idempotent **and** have any authority model bolted on or absent.
Authority is a property of the **delegation/ownership edges** (GFSO Issuer/Executor split, emergent from
`Del`), not a component, seam, invariant, edge-case, failure-mode, or scope-exclusion.

**Why authority is orthogonal (derived, not asserted).** A GFSO decomposition is graded on its
artifact-predicates and how the work splits ({D, Dep, V}) — predicates on *what must be true about the
produced artifact* and *how the work splits*. Who-may-approve a refund/credit is **not** a predicate over the
artifact: a billing rule-set can be perfectly money-conserving, reconciling, and idempotent **and still** have
been operated by any authority model or none. In GFSO's primitives it is an emergent property of the **edges
of the delegation hierarchy**: the **Issuer** sets criteria and validates `V`; the **Executor** produces
(`Del` assigns who, and `V`-by-Issuer ≠ self-validation). It is emergent from `Del` + the delegation
hierarchy, not a node *within* the decomposition.

**Consequences for scoring (scorer rules):**
- A decomposer is **NOT penalized** for omitting any refund-approval / credit-authorization / SoD item —
  there is no decomposition slot it belongs in.
- **Force-fitting it into a criterion (esp. an Invariant) is a category error** (criterion/invariant =
  predicate on the produced amounts; authority = predicate on the process that operates the system). An agent
  who adds "only an admin may issue a refund" earns no criterion-credit and loses none — a correct statement
  in a different plane.
- This is **plane-separation**, not a NEGLECTED scope-exclusion and not a completeness hole.

> **NB (in-plane vs orthogonal).** The *refund amount bound* "a refund can't exceed the captured charge" IS
> in-plane — that is V-I6, a correctness predicate on the amount; only the *who-may-approve* of the refund is
> the orthogonal authority plane.

## 4. Criteria `V` — task predicates (former Invariants + Edge-cases + Failure-modes, COLLAPSED)

> `V` is **derived**: `V(t) = AND(criteria(t))` (§2.2, §3.1 Theorem 1). Every item below is a **predicate of
> the task** — a `criterion` whose conjunction is the validation. The former Invariants (billing-wide spanning
> predicates), Edge-cases (boundary-state predicates) and Failure-modes (defect-coupling predicates) are
> **all criteria** and are collapsed here into one section. Each criterion keeps its verbatim text +
> truth-maker and carries an **FM tag**: which canon failure mode(s) it precludes (§4.2 / §4.6). The old
> I/E/F labels are retired — the FM tag replaces them. (Item ids keep an I-/E-/F- prefix purely for
> cross-reference traceability to `reference_buckets_v1.md`; they are NOT separate categories.)

**How to read the FM tag.** Per §4.2/§4.6: a **spanning/coverage predicate** a faithful decomposition must
carry (and whose omission is a coverage hole) precludes **FM-1** (Correspondence — joint sufficiency); a
predicate enforcing **mutual satisfiability** of children / consistency across a shared base or order
precludes **FM-2** (Consistency); a predicate guarding against an **insensitive / mockable / balanced-but-
wrong** check (a value that passes where reality fails) precludes **FM-3** (Verifiability, false-PASS); a
predicate enforcing **correct fail-propagation / dependency-ordering / termination of the rule** precludes
**FM-4** (Propagation). A small set of T01 criteria carry an **OPERATIONAL** tag (FM-5/6/7) where the
billing task's own retry/dunning/idempotency machinery forces a *design-time* operational predicate (§4.3 of
this doc; §7).

### 4.1 Global correctness criteria (spanning predicates — the billing-wide invariants)

A spanning criterion is a **billing-wide predicate that spans capabilities** — no single capability
establishes it; it holds only if all the coupled capabilities are jointly correct (canon §2.2 joint
sufficiency / §3.1). Canonicalized by the *conserved quantity / enforced predicate*, not by where it is
checked (canon §2.2 non-redundancy). **This is the category T01 is densest in** (PLAN §3 T01: global
money/ledger invariants are where billing genuinely separates).

| # | Name | Conserved quantity / predicate | FM tag (precludes) | Truth-maker (covered iff the decomposition asserts…) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **V-I1** | **Money conservation (no money created or lost across credit ↔ refund ↔ proration)** | Across every event, the money in = money out + balance change; a credit, refund, or proration moves value without creating or destroying it. Sum of (charges − refunds − credits granted + credits consumed) reconciles; no path mints or vaporizes money. | **FM-1** (the defining spanning joint-sufficiency predicate) | …that money is conserved across credit/refund/proration — nothing is created or lost (every credit/refund/proration debit has a matching counterpart). **The defining T01 invariant.** Spans D5, D8, D9, Dep3, Dep4, Dep5. Credit "money in must equal money out / no money created or destroyed." |
| **V-I2** | **Charge idempotency (no double-charge on retry)** | A retried/duplicated charge attempt for the same invoice must not capture money twice — the charge carries an idempotency key (or equivalent dedupe) so a retry after an ambiguous/timed-out result is a no-op if the first succeeded. | **FM-1** (non-redundancy / over-coverage: same charge never captured twice) | …that retrying a charge (dunning, or a timeout/network-ambiguous result) must not double-charge — idempotent charge / dedupe key. Spans D7, Dep9. **Distinct from V-I1** (V-I1 = value conserved across credit/refund/proration; V-I2 = the *same charge* never captured twice). Credit "don't double-charge on retry," "idempotency key on the charge." |
| **V-I3** | **Invoice totals reconcile (line items sum to the charged total, incl. tax & credits)** | The final amount charged equals the sum of its line items: base ± proration − credit + tax, each rounded under one regime (Dep7), with no residual. The invoice is internally consistent; the customer is charged exactly the sum of its stated parts. | **FM-1** (spanning invoice-reconciliation coverage predicate) | …that the invoice line items (base, proration, credit, tax) sum exactly to the charged total (internal reconciliation, no residual). Spans D4, D5, D6, D8, Dep1, Dep4, Dep7. **Distinct from V-I1** (V-I1 = value conserved across *events*; V-I3 = one *invoice* sums correctly). Credit "the invoice total equals the sum of its line items including tax and credits." |
| **V-I4** | **Tax computed at the correct rate-and-base in force at the event time** | Tax uses the rate and jurisdiction applicable **at the moment of the billable event** (charge/refund time), on the correct net base — not a stale rate, not a future rate; a rate change does not retroactively re-tax a past charge. | **FM-1** (tax-at-event-time coverage) + FM-2 (rate/base consistency at the event) | …that tax uses the rate/jurisdiction in force at the event time on the net base (no stale/retroactive rate; tax-at-event-time). Spans D6, Dep1, Dep2. **Distinct from Dep1** (Dep1 = tax base = prorated *amount*; V-I4 = tax rate/base = the one in force *at the event time*). Credit "tax at the rate effective when the charge happens." |
| **V-I5** | **Monotonic / append-only ledger (no silent edit; reversal not deletion)** | Billing records (invoices, charges, credits, refunds) are an append-only ledger: a correction is a new reversing entry, never an in-place edit or deletion of a posted record. History is auditable; no charge silently disappears or mutates. | **FM-1** (records-completeness: no posted record silently dropped/mutated) | …that billing records are append-only / immutable — corrections are reversing entries, not edits/deletes (an auditable ledger). Spans D4, D7, D8, D9, V-I1. **Distinct from V-I1** (V-I1 = the *amounts* conserve; V-I5 = the *records* are not silently mutated). Credit "ledger is append-only," "corrections are new entries, not edits." |
| **V-I6** | **No negative charge except as a refund; refund ≤ amount captured** | A charge to the payment method is never negative (a negative "charge" is a refund and goes through the refund path), and a refund never exceeds what was actually captured for that charge (cumulative refunds ≤ original capture). No over-refund mints money. | **FM-1** (per-charge sign/bound coverage) | …that charges are non-negative (negatives are refunds via the refund path) AND a refund can't exceed the captured amount (no over-refund). Spans D7, D9, Dep5, Dep6. **Distinct from V-I1** (V-I1 = global conservation; V-I6 = the *per-charge* sign/bound that prevents the over-refund leak). Credit "no negative charges / a refund can't exceed what was paid." |
| **V-I7** | **No paid access without a successful captured charge (entitlement ↔ payment coupling)** | A subscription confers paid entitlement only after a charge actually **succeeds** (or a valid trial/credit covers it): a failed/declined charge in dunning does not silently grant or retain paid access beyond the stated grace, and access is revoked at the dunning terminal. | **FM-1** (entitlement↔payment spanning coupling) | …that paid access is granted only on a successful charge (or trial/credit), and a failed charge in dunning doesn't silently keep paid access past grace. Spans D1, D7, Dep9. **Distinct from V-I2** (V-I2 = don't double-charge; V-I7 = don't grant access without a charge). Credit "no service without a successful payment," "revoke on dunning give-up." |
| **V-I8** | **Exactly-once cycle advancement (one billing obligation per period)** | Each billing period advances **exactly once**: a period is billed once and only once — never twice (a duplicate renewal run) and never skipped (a missed period). Simultaneous lifecycle/clock events that touch the same boundary (a cancel and a renewal landing on the same instant, two renewal triggers firing together) are **ordered deterministically** so the period resolves to a single obligation. | **FM-1** (exactly-once per-period coverage) + FM-2 (deterministic ordering of simultaneous boundary events) | …that each period produces exactly one renewal obligation — no period billed twice or skipped, and simultaneous events at a boundary are deterministically ordered to one outcome. Spans D1, D3, D7. **Distinct from Dep8** (Dep8 = no double-charge *across a transition*; V-I8 = exactly-once *per period* + the simultaneous-event ordering rule) and **from V-I2** (V-I2 = same *charge* not captured twice; V-I8 = same *period* not advanced twice). Credit "bill each cycle exactly once," "renewal is idempotent per period," "order concurrent renewal/cancel deterministically." |

> **Criteria vs capabilities.** The invariant criteria are *spanning predicates*; the seams (`Dep`) are the
> *capability couplings* that must be right for the predicate to hold. They are scored separately (canon §2.2
> non-redundancy): an agent asserting the *predicate* "money is conserved" (V-I1) without naming the
> *refund-routing* or *plan-change-proration* seams (Dep5, Dep3) has covered V-I1 not those `Dep`, and vice
> versa. The invariant criteria canonicalize by the conserved quantity (money-conservation ≠ charge-idempotency
> ≠ invoice-reconciliation ≠ tax-at-event-time ≠ append-only-ledger ≠ no-over-refund ≠ entitlement-coupling ≠
> exactly-once-cycle-advancement — eight genuinely distinct predicates; a design can satisfy any subset and
> fail another).

### 4.2 Boundary-state criteria (former Edge-cases)

A boundary-state criterion is a boundary input/state that a naive happy-path design silently mishandles —
i.e. a predicate the design must satisfy at the boundary, or the corresponding check is **insensitive** there
(passes a wrong result). Covered iff the decomposition names the same boundary condition and that it needs a
rule.

| # | Name | What it requires | FM tag (precludes) | Truth-maker (covered iff named) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **V-E1** | **Mid-period change exactly on the period boundary** | A plan change (or cancel) landing *exactly* on the renewal instant: the design must define whether the change belongs to the old or new period (no double proration, no zero-day window double-charge), at a single decision point. | **FM-3** (boundary-instant check insensitive → double proration/charge passes silently) | …the change-on-the-period-boundary case and a single old-vs-new-period rule (no double proration / double charge at the boundary instant). Credit "what if the change happens exactly at renewal." |
| **V-E2** | **Multiple changes in one cycle (stacked prorations)** | More than one plan change within a single billing period: each proration must compose correctly so the net is conserved (not re-crediting an already-credited remainder, not compounding rounding). The design must define proration against the *current* effective state, not the period start each time. | **FM-3** (happy-path check insensitive to stacked prorations → re-credit/compound passes) | …the multiple-changes-in-one-cycle case and that stacked prorations compose without re-crediting / compounding (net conserved). Credit "two upgrades in the same month." **Distinct from Dep3** (Dep3 = one change; V-E2 = the *stacking* of several). |
| **V-E3** | **Partial-period proration of a sub-day / very short remainder** | Proration of a tiny remaining fraction (a few hours/days, or a switch on the last day): the basis and rounding must yield a sensible, conserved amount (not a rounding artifact that over/under-charges, not a negative remainder). | **FM-3** (a value check insensitive to a sub-day rounding artifact → over/under-charge passes) | …the very-short-remainder proration case and that the basis/rounding stays conserved (no rounding artifact). Credit "prorate a partial day / last-day change." Ties to V-F4. |
| **V-E4** | **Tax-exempt customer / zero-rate jurisdiction** | A customer/jurisdiction with no tax (tax-exempt entity, zero-rate region, reverse-charge B2B): the tax line is zero/absent by rule, not by a failed lookup, and the invoice still reconciles (V-I3). | **FM-3** (an accidental/failed-lookup zero passes as a legitimate zero-tax) | …the tax-exempt / zero-rate case and a defined zero-tax rule (not an accidental/failed-lookup zero). Credit "tax-exempt customer," "B2B reverse charge / zero-rated region." |
| **V-E5** | **Trial → paid conversion (and trial with no charge)** | The trial period bills nothing (or a $0 invoice), and conversion at trial end triggers the first real charge on the correct anchor (Dep9). Includes early-cancel-during-trial (no charge owed) and trial-with-a-required-card vs no-card. | **FM-3** (a happy-path check insensitive to the trial/no-charge boundary → charges in trial or mis-dates conversion) | …the trial→paid boundary: no charge during trial, first charge at conversion on the right date, and cancel-during-trial owes nothing. Credit "trial converts to paid and bills then," "cancel during trial = no charge." |
| **V-E6** | **Zero-amount / fully-credited invoice** | An invoice whose net is **zero** (credit ≥ charge, or a $0 plan, or 100% coupon): the design must not submit a $0/negative charge to the gateway (V-I6), must still post the invoice and draw down credit (Dep4), and must grant entitlement (V-I7) without a capture. | **FM-3** (a zero/negative-total check insensitive to the fully-credited case → $0 charge submitted or access denied) | …the zero-amount / fully-credited invoice case and that no $0 charge is submitted yet the invoice posts, credit draws down, and access is granted. Credit "invoice nets to zero / fully covered by credit." **Distinct from V-E4** (V-E4 = zero *tax*; V-E6 = zero *total*). |
| **V-E7** | **Currency / rounding boundary (sub-cent, smallest-unit, currency with no minor unit)** | Amounts at the rounding edge — sub-cent proration slices, a zero-decimal currency (JPY), banker's-vs-half-up at exactly .5 — must follow the stated regime (Dep7) so parts still reconcile to the total (V-I3) with no leaked fraction. | **FM-3** (a balance check insensitive to a sub-unit/zero-decimal boundary → leaked fraction passes) | …the rounding/smallest-currency-unit boundary and that the stated regime keeps parts reconciling to the total. Credit "round to the currency's smallest unit," "zero-decimal currency," "half-cent handling." **Distinct from Dep7** (Dep7 = *consistency* of the regime across line items; V-E7 = the *boundary values* the regime must define). |
| **V-E8** | **Cancel/refund/reactivate timing & ordering corners (incl. backdated change)** | Reactivation after a refund-on-cancel (don't re-grant a refunded period for free), a refund issued after a downgrade-credit (don't refund the already-credited remainder twice), cancel during dunning (stop retries cleanly), upgrade-vs-downgrade timing (upgrade effective now vs downgrade effective next period, per stated policy). A **backdated change** (a change effective-dated into an already-finalized period) does not mutate the finalized invoice (V-I5) but issues a **correcting credit/charge memo** for the over/under-billed delta. | **FM-3** (happy-path insensitive to the ordering corner → re-grant/double-refund passes) + FM-4 (a late/backdated event must propagate to a correcting memo, not mutate the finalized invoice) | …a cancel/refund/reactivate ordering corner and a defined rule (no refunded-period re-grant, no double-refund of a credited remainder, cancel-during-dunning stops retries, upgrade-now-vs-downgrade-next-period, **backdated change → correcting memo not invoice mutation**). Credit any concrete timing corner among these with a stated resolution. **Distinct from Dep8** (Dep8 = the per-transition billing consequence; V-E8 = the *interaction/ordering* of two such events). |

### 4.3 Defect-coupling criteria (former Failure-modes — only those with NO `Dep` twin)

Phrased as **concrete coverable claims** (PLAN §1.3 rule 4), never adjectives. Each names a specific defect
coupling AND its resolution; covered iff the decomposition names the same coupling and a guard.
**One-defect-one-place (PLAN §1.3 rule 1):** a defect-coupling is kept here ONLY if it has **no `Dep`-seam or
invariant-criterion twin**. A defect that shares the *same pair, same falsifier, same fix* as an existing
seam/invariant is **not** a separate item — the seam/invariant already carries it. The obvious billing
failures are **deliberately NOT listed** because they are already carried: *double-charge on retry* = V-I2;
*lost/dropped credit* = Dep4/V-I1; *tax on the wrong base* = Dep1/Dep2/V-I4; *refund exceeding the charge* =
V-I6; *over-charge by dropping the old-plan proration credit* = Dep3/V-I1; *credit lost on a failed card leg*
= Dep10. What remains here are couplings with **no** seam/invariant twin, each pinned to a canon §4 FM class.

| # | Name | FM tag (precludes) | What it requires (coupling + guard) | Truth-maker (covered iff named with a guard) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **V-F1** | **Ambiguous charge result → double-charge or lost capture (the retry-without-confirmation coupling)** | **FM-7** (no feedback/reconciliation channel) — *the operational mode the billing task forces at design time, see §7* | A charge attempt that *times out* or returns ambiguously (the gateway captured but the response was lost) must be **reconciled** before retry — otherwise dunning either double-charges (retry of a succeeded charge) or treats a real capture as failed (lost money). **The idempotency-key predicate V-I2 prevents the double-charge mechanically; V-F1 is the missing *reconciliation/confirmation channel* that resolves the ambiguous outcome** (query the gateway / webhook reconciliation). | …that an ambiguous/timed-out charge result needs reconciliation (gateway query / webhook) before retrying, not a blind retry. **Distinct from V-I2** (V-I2 = the idempotency *key* that makes a retry safe; V-F1 = the *channel* that determines whether the first attempt actually captured). Credit "reconcile an uncertain charge before retrying," "webhook to confirm capture." |
| **V-F2** | **Stale / drifted plan or rate snapshot priced a live invoice (no point-in-time pinning)** | **FM-5** (staleness — contract evaluated against stale inputs) | A renewal/charge that prices against a **changed** plan price or tax rate without pinning the version in force at the event makes a charge silently use the wrong number (a price change mid-cycle re-prices a period the customer already committed to). Guard: pin the plan-version / rate-version at invoice generation (point-in-time snapshot), so a later catalog change doesn't retro-price. | …that a plan-price or tax-rate change must not retro-price an already-anchored invoice — pin the version at invoice time. **Distinct from V-I4** (V-I4 = tax-*rate*-at-event-time as a correctness predicate; V-F2 = the *staleness mechanism* — a mid-cycle catalog/price change drifting into a live invoice — across *plan price too*, with point-in-time pinning as the guard). Credit "snapshot the price/rate at billing time," "grandfather the committed period." |
| **V-F3** | **Dunning never terminates / infinite retry (no give-up terminal, no entitlement revocation)** | **FM-6** (non-terminating / undefined terminal) | A dunning loop with no defined give-up condition retries forever (or indefinitely retains paid access in past_due) — the retry schedule must have a **bounded terminal** (max attempts / max days → cancel/expire) that revokes entitlement (ties to V-I7). Guard: a stated max-retry/max-age terminal that transitions to canceled and stops billing. | …that dunning must have a bounded give-up terminal (max attempts/days → cancel + revoke access), not retry indefinitely. **Distinct from V-I7** (V-I7 = no paid access without a successful charge as a predicate; V-F3 = the dunning *loop's termination* — the FM-6 non-terminating defect). Credit "dunning gives up after N attempts and cancels," "bounded retry schedule." |
| **V-F4** | **Rounding leak across split line items (sum-of-rounded ≠ rounded-of-sum)** | **FM-1** → manifests as a V-I3 violation (kept as a distinct defect-coupling per the residual-cents allocation it requires) | Rounding each line item independently (base, proration, credit, tax) can make the rounded parts **not sum** to the rounded total — a recurring sub-cent leak that, at scale, loses or mints money. Guard: a **defined residual-allocation rule** (largest-remainder / penny-allocation: compute the total once, distribute rounding residue to a designated line) so parts reconcile to the total (V-I3) and nothing leaks. | …that independently rounding line items can break reconciliation (sum-of-rounded ≠ total) AND a residual-allocation/penny-rounding rule that closes the gap. **Distinct from Dep7/V-I3** (Dep7 = *one* currency/regime; V-I3 = parts *do* sum; **V-F4 = the concrete *residual-cent allocation* mechanism that makes V-I3 hold under independent rounding** — the guard, not just the predicate). Credit "allocate the rounding remainder so line items reconcile," "largest-remainder rounding." **Flag for scorer:** if an agent collapses V-F4 into V-I3/Dep7, credit one and note the merge — see Appendix. |

> **Defect-coupling discipline.** Only four defect-coupling criteria survive because T01 is *invariant-and-seam
> dense*: most concrete failures ARE the negation of a seam (`Dep`) or invariant (V-I) and are scored there,
> not double-counted (PLAN §0 residual concern: F-vs-X double-counting explicitly flagged — enforced here).
> The §2.2-named failure-modes map cleanly: *double-charge on retry* → **V-I2** (predicate) + **V-F1** (the
> reconciliation channel that resolves the ambiguous case); *lost/dropped credit* → **Dep4/V-I1**; *tax on the
> wrong base* → **Dep1/Dep2/V-I4**; *proration rounding that leaks money* → **V-F4** (the un-twinned residual-
> allocation guard); *refund exceeding charge* → **V-I6**; *credit lost on a failed card leg* → **Dep10**.
> V-F1–V-F4 are the couplings with **no** clean seam/invariant twin (an ambiguous-result *channel*; a
> *staleness* mechanism over plan price; a non-terminating *dunning loop*; a residual-cent *allocation* rule).
> FM-2 (incompatible child criteria) and FM-3 (silent false-PASS) couplings are carried by the I-invariants
> (V-I1/V-I3) and the boundary criteria (V-E1–V-E8) respectively, not duplicated here.

> **Note on the operational FM tags.** V-F1 (FM-7), V-F2 (FM-5), and V-F3 (FM-6) name OPERATIONAL failure
> modes at design time — because the *billing task itself* (failed charges / retries / dunning / idempotency
> are in the verbatim capability list) forces a designed reconciliation channel (V-F1), a point-in-time snapshot
> against mid-run catalog drift (V-F2), and a bounded dunning terminal (V-F3). The mode each guards is a runtime
> one, but the requirement is a static design predicate the task statement forces. This does NOT make the static
> reference responsible for the live-runtime remainder of FM-5/6/7 (re-derivation on spec mutation, escalation
> signalling) — see §7.

## 5. NEGLECTED — declared scope-exclusions (each with its `predictability_verdict`, STD-1/2/3)

> Canon §5.1 STD-1: a decomposition **without** NEGLECTED is incomplete by definition — the auditable "no
> silent skip" record. Each entry carries a **`predictability_verdict`** (STD-2 §5.2: predictability is
> presumed; the *declarer* bears the burden of proving safe-to-skip) and an `invalidation_condition` (when the
> exclusion flips in-scope). Only assumptions whose **omission would otherwise be a foreseeable defect**
> (FM-1.b, §5.2 / §4.2: a predictable external risk left without a mitigating child) — assessed-and-declared-
> skipped, not a dumping ground (PLAN §0 N-inflation caution): an exclusion is listed only if omitting it makes
> an *external* decision look like a billing-logic bug. **T01's other dense category** (PLAN §3 T01: the
> NEGLECTED policy boundaries are where billing separates).

| # | Excluded assumption | `predictability_verdict` (why safely excluded) | Truth-maker (covered iff the decomposition declares…) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **N1** | **Proration/rounding *policy* is a stated input, not derived** | The *choice* of proration basis (time-based vs usage-based) and rounding rule (half-up / banker's / largest-remainder) is a business-policy input the billing logic *applies*, not one it can derive. Declared boundary: which policy is correct is a product/finance decision; the logic must take it as a parameter and apply it consistently (Dep7) and allocate residuals (V-F4). Invalidation: if the policy is undefined, proration becomes ambiguous and rounding leaks (V-F4) re-enter as in-scope defects. `predictability_verdict`: S-regularity exists (every billing system parameterizes this) → declared boundary, **provided** the consistent-application (Dep7) and residual (V-F4) machinery is in-scope. | …that the proration basis & rounding *policy* is an assumed/parameterized input (not derived by the logic). |
| **N2** | **Tax rates / jurisdiction tables / nexus are supplied by an external tax engine** | The authoritative tax **rate, jurisdiction resolution, and nexus determination** come from an external source (a tax service / rate table). Declared boundary: a wrong rate from the engine is an external-dependency error, not a billing-logic defect; the logic's job is to apply the *supplied* rate to the *correct base* (Dep1/Dep2) at the *correct time* (V-I4). Invalidation: if rate determination is in-scope (the logic must compute jurisdiction/nexus), tax-rate-sourcing becomes a primary requirement. `predictability_verdict`: S-regularity exists (tax engines are the industry norm) → declared boundary. | …that tax rates / jurisdiction / nexus are supplied by an external tax engine (the logic applies, not determines, the rate). |
| **N3** | **Payment gateway capture/settlement mechanics are assumed (the logic computes the amount, the gateway moves the money)** | The billing logic computes *what* to charge and *when*; the gateway's capture/auth/settlement, PCI handling, and 3DS/SCA flows are downstream. Declared boundary: a gateway outage/decline is surfaced (and drives dunning via D7) but the gateway's internal correctness is assumed; "the money moved" is the gateway's contract. Invalidation: if the logic must implement settlement/auth itself (acting as the processor), capture mechanics become in-scope. `predictability_verdict`: S-regularity exists (gateways are universal) → declared boundary. **Note:** the *ambiguous-result reconciliation* is NOT excluded — it is in-scope as V-F1 (omitting it misattributes a real double-charge to "the gateway"). | …that the payment gateway's capture/settlement/PCI/SCA mechanics are assumed external (the logic computes the amount, not the money movement). |
| **N4** | **Revenue recognition / accounting (GAAP/IFRS) is out of scope** | How billed revenue is *recognized* over time (deferred revenue, ASC 606 / IFRS 15 schedules) is a downstream accounting concern, distinct from *what to charge and when*. Declared boundary: a rev-rec discrepancy is an accounting-system concern, not a billing-charge defect; the billing logic emits charges/credits, the GL recognizes them. Invalidation: if the task required producing rev-rec schedules, deferred-revenue logic becomes in-scope. `predictability_verdict`: S-regularity exists (rev-rec is a separate system layer) → declared boundary. | …that revenue recognition / deferred-revenue accounting (GAAP/IFRS, ASC 606) is out of scope (billing emits charges; the GL recognizes). |
| **N5** | **Fraud / chargeback / disputes are out of scope** | Fraud detection, chargeback handling, and payment disputes are a risk/operations layer beyond the billing-calculation logic. Declared boundary: a fraudulent charge or a bank chargeback is a disputes-process concern; the billing logic's correctness (right amount, conserved money) is independent of whether a charge is later disputed. Invalidation: if chargeback-driven reversals must flow back into the ledger automatically, dispute handling (and its money-conservation impact, V-I1) becomes in-scope. `predictability_verdict`: S-regularity exists (fraud/disputes are a distinct domain) → declared boundary. | …that fraud / chargeback / dispute handling is out of scope (the calculation logic assumes a charge is genuine). |
| **N6** | **Coupons / promotional discounts are out of scope (distinct from account credits)** | The verbatim task lists **"account credits / balance"** (a stored, conserved customer balance — D8/Dep4) but **not** coupons or promotional discounts (percent-off / fixed-off codes applied at checkout, with their own discount-before-tax ordering and stacking rules). Declared boundary: a coupon engine is a separate pricing-adjustment capability whose absence isn't a billing-correctness bug for the in-scope capabilities — account-credit application (Dep4) and credit-vs-tax ordering (Dep2) are *not* the coupon path. Invalidation: **if the spec adds coupons/promo codes, a `discount` component plus a `discount-before-tax` ordering seam (discount reduces the taxable base before tax, distinct from the credit-vs-tax order Dep2) and a discount×proration interaction become in-scope** (the discount-before-tax order is a real money-bug). `predictability_verdict`: S-regularity exists (coupon engines are a standard *separate* capability) AND the task text excludes it → declared boundary, not a forgotten requirement. | …that coupons / promotional discounts are out of scope (only stored *account credits* are in-scope), **or** — if the decomposer reads them in — a discount component with a stated discount-before-tax order. |

> **N-discipline (STD-1/2/3).** Each N is an assumption whose *failure* would otherwise be misattributed to the
> billing logic (omitting N1 makes a "rounding leak" look like a logic bug when the *policy* was never
> specified; omitting N2 makes a "wrong tax" look like a billing bug when the external *rate* was wrong;
> omitting N3 makes a "gateway decline/outage" look like a charge-computation defect; omitting N4 makes a
> "rev-rec discrepancy" look like a billing error; omitting N5 makes a "chargeback/fraud loss" look like a
> money-conservation defect; omitting N6 makes a "missing coupon engine" look like a billing-logic gap when
> coupons are simply outside the task's capability list). Each carries a `predictability_verdict` and an
> invalidation trigger. **Six** assumptions whose omission is a foreseeable *misattribution* or scope-confusion
> (N1 rounding/proration policy, N2 tax engine, N3 gateway mechanics, N4 revenue recognition, N5
> fraud/chargebacks, N6 coupons/promotional discounts). Excluded from N (handled in-band, not new exclusions):
> "dunning policy specifics / retry schedule" (the *terminal* is in-scope as V-F3; the specific cadence is a

## 6. Hardest / most-easily-forgotten seams (flag for scorer)

> Not a category — a scorer pointer at the highest-signal `Dep` edges.

- **Dep1 — tax on the *prorated* amount (tax base = net, not gross).** The single most-forgotten T01 seam:
  proration and tax are designed as separate components and tax silently runs on the full plan price on a
  mid-cycle change. A parts-list names "proration" and "tax" but drops the *base* one feeds the other.
- **Dep8 — lifecycle transition × proration/anchor (pause/resume's effect on the renewal date).** Pause/resume
  shifting the billing anchor (and cancel's prorate-or-run-to-end) is routinely dropped; the state machine and
  the proration engine are designed apart and the period-accounting across the transition is forgotten.
- **Dep6 — a refund reverses the proportional *tax* portion, not just the base.** Refund is designed against
  the charge total; the tax split of a partial refund is the classic dropped sub-seam (over/under-refunds tax).
- **Dep3 — mid-cycle plan change must credit the *old* plan, not only charge the new.** Dropping the
  unused-old proration credit silently over-charges every upgrade/downgrade — a money-conservation (V-I1)
  violation.
- **(Honorable mention, V-F4) — rounding-residual allocation across split line items.** Independently rounding
  base/proration/credit/tax makes sum-of-rounded ≠ total; a recurring sub-cent leak. Often dropped because the
  single-line happy path reconciles trivially and the multi-line residue is invisible until scale.
