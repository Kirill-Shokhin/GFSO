## Task `T` (verbatim, PLAN §3 T04)

> Design the logic of a month-end close for a company operating in several currencies:
> sub-ledger→general-ledger posting, FX revaluation, intercompany elimination, accruals & deferrals,
> bank/sub-ledger reconciliation, and producing a trial balance that nets to zero. Specify the rules and
> their ordering so the close is correct and auditable.

**Shape.** Hub-and-spoke: the general ledger / trial balance is the **hub**; each spoke (sub-ledgers,
FX revaluation, intercompany elimination, accruals/deferrals, reconciliation) feeds it. The hub-level
invariant (debits = credits, nets to zero, per currency and consolidated) constrains all spokes jointly.
Achievement goal: a correct, ordered rule-set that *terminates* in a signed-off trial balance — not a
maintenance regime.

**Single meta-level.** This decomposes the close **logic** at one level: the rules and their ordering.
It does NOT spawn departmental sub-projects (staffing the AP team, building the GL system, running the
audit). Components are passes of one design, not org units. (Consequence: the OPERATIONAL failure modes
{FM-5,6,7} — runtime/execution-time modes — are mostly out of scope for this static single-meta-level
decomposition; see §7.)

## 1. Subtasks `D` — the close passes / spokes + hub

> `D` = the children of the decomposition (canon §2.2: `D: T → P(T)`, a DAG).

Each subtask is a **pass** in the close: a separable region of work with its own pass/fail criterion on
its *output state* (a posted, balanced set of entries), not a self-report ("AP closed"). Sub-ledger close
is treated as ONE subtask (the spokes feeding it are sources, §D1 truth-maker), to keep the design at one
meta-level and avoid inflating `D` with every source system.

| # | Name | What it requires | Truth-maker (covered iff the decomposition names…) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **D1** | **Sub-ledger close & cutoff** | Each sub-ledger (AP, AR, payroll, inventory/fixed-assets, cash/bank) is closed for the period: all in-period transactions captured, post-cutoff transactions excluded, sub-ledger frozen. | …a pass that closes/freezes the operational sub-ledgers and fixes the period cutoff for what they contain, BEFORE they post up. Any phrasing that the source ledgers must be final/frozen before posting. |
| **D2** | **Sub-ledger → GL posting** | The frozen sub-ledger balances are summarized and posted to the general ledger as balanced journal entries (each entry debits = credits). | …a posting/transfer pass that moves sub-ledger totals into the GL as journal entries. Credit if they name "post sub-ledgers to GL" or "journalize sub-ledger activity," however worded. |
| **D3** | **Adjusting entries — accruals & deferrals AND estimates/provisions** | Adjusting entries of two sub-classes: **(a) timing** — accrue earned-not-billed / incurred-not-paid; defer billed-not-earned / prepaid; their reversal next period; **(b) judgmental estimates/provisions** — bad-debt allowance, warranty, rebate, bonus and similar reserves rolled to the current period on a documented basis. | **D4** | **FX revaluation & realized-FX recognition** | Re-measure foreign-currency-denominated monetary balances at the period-end rate and book the **unrealized** FX gain/loss; **AND** on items that settled in-period, recognize the **realized** FX gain/loss at the settlement rate; (if consolidating) translate each entity's statements into the reporting currency. (The realized-FX producer must appear, or the Dep11 netting seam has nothing to attach to.) |
| **D5** | **Consolidation eliminations — intercompany AND investment/equity + NCI** | Two distinct elimination mechanisms on consolidation: **(a) intercompany** — identify and eliminate IC transactions/balances (IC AP↔AR, IC sales/COGS, IC loans) so only third-party amounts remain; **(b) investment-vs-equity + NCI** — eliminate the parent's investment against subsidiary equity, and for <100%-owned subs compute non-controlling (minority) interest = minority% × subsidiary net assets, so consolidated equity ties. Credit "bank rec," "tie out sub-ledger to GL control," "reconcile and clear differences." |

Credit "produce trial balance," "close the books," "final TB and lock." This is the **hub**. |
| **D8** | **Unrealized intercompany profit-in-inventory elimination** | Profit on intercompany sales sitting in ending inventory (or fixed assets) is eliminated until realized externally, so consolidated assets carry no IC mark-up. A **distinct** elimination from D5: D5 removes IC balances/P&L double-counts; D8 removes margin *embedded in a retained asset* that no external sale has yet realized. | …an elimination of intercompany profit retained in ending inventory/fixed assets (consolidated inventory carries no IC mark-up; the mark-up is deferred until external realization). > **Note on `D` vs hub.** D7 is both a subtask (a pass that *runs*) and the locus of the global invariant

> internal checks (one-defect-one-place; canon §2.2 non-redundancy). D8 (IC profit-in-inventory) is kept a
> separate subtask rather than folded into D5 because its truth-maker is distinct (margin embedded in a
> retained asset, not an IC balance double-count) — an agent can cover D5 in full and still miss D8.

## 2. Dependencies `Dep` — cross-subtask edges as explicit, separately-falsifiable `Dep` edges

> `Dep` = the canon primitive (§2.2): an acyclic relation on `T × T`; `(tₐ, tᵦ) ∈ Dep` iff `criteria(tᵦ)`
> references the output of `tₐ` (with coherence `deadline(tₐ) < deadline(tᵦ)`). **Each `Dep` edge is a
> criterion of a child that references a sibling's output — the glue** — and carries its **own** falsifying
> truth-maker (the anti-mock condition), not "these passes relate" prose.

**The most important and most-forgotten category.** Each seam is a REAL edge: the *output of one named
component feeds the input of another*, and the edge carries its **own** falsifying criterion — not "these
passes relate" prose (Constitution Ст. II.5 anti-mock). T04's seams are of two kinds: **(a) spoke→hub
posting seams** (a spoke's result must actually land in the GL/TB) and **(b) cross-spoke ORDERING seams**
(pass A must complete before pass B or the result is wrong — the dominant, richest content here). A seam is
covered iff the decomposition names the **same ordered pair** and the **same thing that breaks** if the
edge is absent/violated.

| # | Name | Pair (source → consumer) | What the edge requires | Truth-maker (covered iff named with its breakage) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Dep0** | **Opening-balance roll-forward from prior close** *(most fundamental seam — listed first)* | prior-period closed TB → current-period opening GL | The period does **not** start from zero: current-period opening balances ARE the prior period's signed-off closing balances — balance-sheet accounts roll forward, P&L accounts reset to zero into retained earnings. If the opening GL does not tie to the prior close (or the P&L→retained-earnings close-out is skipped), the TB is wrong from t=0 regardless of how perfectly every spoke runs. Strictly larger than the accrual-reversal carry (Dep10). | …that the period opens from the prior period's signed-off closing balances — B/S accounts roll forward and P&L closes out to retained earnings — and that a mismatch to the prior close corrupts the TB before any spoke runs. Distinct ordered pair (prior-TB → opening-GL), distinct falsifier (opening ≠ prior close). **Not a re-skin of Dep10** (Dep10 carries only the accrual reversals; Dep0 carries the *entire* opening balance sheet + the equity close-out). |
| **Dep1** | **Cutoff precedes posting** | D1 → D2 | Sub-ledgers must be frozen/cutoff-fixed *before* they post to GL; posting an un-frozen ledger lets post-cutoff or still-changing entries leak in. | …that posting must happen only after sub-ledger cutoff/freeze, and that posting an open ledger corrupts the GL. (Ordering edge + the leak it prevents.) |
| **Dep2** | **Sub-ledger control reconciles to GL** | D2 ↔ D6 | The GL control-account balance for each sub-ledger must equal the sub-ledger's own detail total; the rec consumes both and a mismatch is a real defect. | …that the GL control account is tied back to the sub-ledger detail (subledger total = GL control). The shared artifact is the control-account balance. |
| **Dep3** | **Accruals/deferrals posted before TB** | D3 → D7 (via GL) | Period-cutoff adjusting entries must be posted into the GL *before* the trial balance is struck, or the TB reports the wrong period. | …that adjusting entries land in the GL before the TB is assembled; a TB struck before adjustments is wrong/incomplete. |
| **Dep4** | **Reconciliation clears before close** | D6 → D7 | Open reconciling items (unmatched bank/sub-ledger differences) must be resolved (or booked as known adjustments) *before* sign-off; an un-cleared rec means the TB hides an error. | …that recs must be cleared/resolved before the period is locked; closing over open reconciling items is a defect. |

| **Dep6** | **Intercompany nets before trial balance** | D5 → D7 | Intercompany eliminations must be applied *before* the consolidated trial balance is struck; eliminating after TB leaves the consolidated TB double-counted (and possibly non-zero). | …that IC elimination is applied before the consolidated TB. **Hard ordering seam.** Credit "eliminate then consolidate," "TB after eliminations." |
| **Dep7** | **IC balances match before elimination (two-sided)** | D5 internal-cross-entity | Each side of an intercompany pair (entity A's IC-AR vs entity B's IC-AP) must agree *before* elimination; a one-sided/unmatched IC balance leaves a residual that breaks the consolidated zero. | …that the two legs of each intercompany pair must reconcile/agree before being eliminated; an unmatched IC pair leaves a residual. Shared artifact = the IC pair balance across two entities. |

Shared artifact = the rate table instance. (If two passes read different tables, the books don't tie.) |
| **Dep9** | **Posting precedes revaluation** | D2 → D4 | FX revaluation operates on the *posted* GL foreign balances; revaluing before sub-ledger activity is posted revalues a stale/incomplete balance. | …that revaluation runs on the posted GL balances (after D2), i.e. post first, then revalue. > **Anti-mock note.** Dep2, Dep7, Dep8, Dep11 reference a concrete *shared artifact* (control-account
> balance, IC pair balance, rate table, the FX exposure) — a forgotten seam here is an objectively
> identifiable defect (the books won't tie), not a nominal pair. Ordering / cross-period seams (Dep0, Dep1,
> Dep3, Dep4, Dep5, Dep6, Dep9, Dep10) are falsified by a concrete *out-of-order* (or opening-mismatch)
> execution that yields a wrong number. None of these collapse onto each other: different ordered pair OR
> different falsifier ⇒ distinct seam (canon §2.2 non-redundancy). In particular Dep0 (whole opening balance
> sheet rolls forward) ≠ Dep10 (only this-period accruals reverse) — strictly larger pair, different
> falsifier.

> **FM mapping for `Dep`.** A forgotten/violated `Dep` edge is an **FM-1** defect (a spanning integration
> criterion — the glue — not written / not covered; §4.2 FM-1 insufficiency, §18.10 *hole-i*) when it is a
> coverage hole, and an **FM-2** defect (criteria of two children incompatible / a relational defect at
> fixed composition; §4.2 FM-2) for the two-sided / shared-artifact seams whose breakage is a mutual-
> satisfiability conflict (Dep2, Dep7, Dep8, Dep11). The §7 FM table records this. Per one-defect-one-place,
> a seam's failure is scored **here as a `Dep` item**, never re-listed as a separate criterion.

## 3. Authority `Del` — orthogonal note (NOT scored)

> **`Del` is the canon "who" axis** (§2.3: `Del: T → A`, exactly one agent per task) — **orthogonal** to the
> decomposition plane `{D, Dep, V}` and **NOT scored** as a decomposition item. This is a plane-separation
> note, NOT a NEGLECTED exclusion, NOT a criterion, NOT a completeness hole.

The goal is "correct **and auditable**." Auditability has **three** legs, but they do not all live in the
same plane:

1. **Lineage** — every posted TB figure traces to a source document/journal entry — *in the decomposition
   plane* (carried by **V-I7** leg-1).
2. **Reproducibility** — same inputs → same TB — *in the decomposition plane* (carried by **V-I7** leg-2).
3. **Control / segregation-of-duties** — preparer ≠ approver, authorization levels, approval of manual
   JEs — **NOT in the decomposition plane.** It lies in the **ORTHOGONAL AUTHORITY PLANE.**

**Why authority is orthogonal (derived, not asserted).** A GFSO decomposition is graded on its
artifact-predicates and how the work splits ({D, Dep, V}) — predicates on *what must be true about the
produced artifact* and *how the work splits*. Segregation-of-duties is **not** a predicate over the
artifact: a trial balance can be perfectly balanced, per-currency conserved, fully lineage-traced, and
fully reproducible **and still** have been prepared and approved by the same person. SoD is therefore a
property of the **process / authority assignment** (`Del`), not of the produced TB or of any decomposition
node. In GFSO's primitives it is an emergent property of the **edges of the delegation hierarchy**: the
**Issuer** sets criteria and validates `V`; the **Executor** produces (`Del` assigns who, and `V`-by-Issuer
≠ self-validation). "Preparer ≠ approver" is exactly the domain's boundary-term for the **Issuer ≠
Executor-on-validation** separation that the GFSO protocol **already guarantees structurally** (canon
§«Issuer/Executor»: self-validation — one context that both produces and validates — violates IC; CHALLENGE
is meaningless if you contest yourself). It is emergent from `Del` + the delegation hierarchy, not a node
*within* the decomposition.

**Consequences for scoring (scorer rules):**
- A decomposer is **NOT penalized** for failing to list SoD as a decomposition item. SoD is not a subtask,
  dependency, criterion, or scope-exclusion — there is no decomposition slot it belongs in.
- **Force-fitting SoD into a criterion is a category error.** A criterion is a predicate on the produced
  artifact (debits = credits, nets to zero); SoD is a predicate on the *process/authority that produced it*.
  An agent who writes "preparer ≠ approver" earns no criterion-credit and loses none — it is simply a
  correct statement in a different plane.
- This is **plane-separation**, not a NEGLECTED scope-exclusion (it is not "out of scope" — it is *carried
  structurally by the protocol's Issuer/Executor split*) and not a completeness hole (nothing is missing
  from the decomposition).

## 4. Criteria `V` — task predicates (former Invariants + Edge-cases + Failure-modes, COLLAPSED)

> `V` is **derived**: `V(t) = AND(criteria(t))` (§2.2, §3.1 Theorem 1). Every item below is a **predicate of
> the task** — a `criterion` whose conjunction is the validation. The former Invariants (parent-level
> spanning predicates), Edge-cases (boundary-state predicates) and Failure-modes (defect-coupling
> predicates) are **all criteria** and are collapsed here into one section. Each criterion keeps its
> verbatim text + truth-maker and carries an **FM tag**: which canon failure mode(s) it precludes (§4.2 /
> §4.6). The old I/E/F labels are retired — the FM tag replaces them. (Item ids keep an I-/E-/F- prefix
> purely for cross-reference traceability to `reference_buckets_v1.md`; they are NOT separate categories.)

**How to read the FM tag.** Per §4.2/§4.6: a **spanning/coverage predicate** a faithful decomposition must
carry (and whose omission is a coverage hole) precludes **FM-1** (Correspondence — joint sufficiency); a
predicate enforcing **mutual satisfiability** of children / per-currency-vs-aggregate consistency precludes
**FM-2** (Consistency); a predicate guarding against an **insensitive / mockable / balanced-but-wrong**
check (a value that passes where reality fails) precludes **FM-3** (Verifiability, false-PASS); a predicate
enforcing **correct fail-propagation / dependency-ordering** precludes **FM-4** (Propagation).

### 4.1 Global correctness criteria (spanning predicates — the hub constraint)

A spanning criterion is a parent-level predicate that **spans children** — no single spoke can establish it;
it holds only if all spokes are jointly correct (canon §2.2 joint sufficiency / §3.1). Canonicalized by the
*conserved quantity / enforced predicate*, not by where it is checked (one item per category).

| **V-I2** | **Currency conservation (per-currency balance)** | The books balance **within each transaction/functional currency** separately, not only in the consolidated total — a cross-currency posting must balance per currency at its booked rate. | **FM-2** (per-currency vs aggregate consistency) | …that balance holds *per currency*, not just in aggregate. **Distinct from V-I1**: an entry can net to zero in reporting currency while being unbalanced per currency. ### 4.2 Boundary-state criteria (former Edge-cases)

A boundary-state criterion is a boundary input/state that a naive happy-path design silently mishandles —
i.e. a predicate the design must satisfy at the boundary, or the corresponding check is **insensitive**
there (passes a wrong result). Covered iff the decomposition names the same boundary condition and that it
needs a rule.

| # | Name | What it requires | FM tag (precludes) | Truth-maker (covered iff named) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **V-E1** | **Rate-at-cutoff vs spot/average mismatch** | Which rate applies to which item type (closing rate for monetary B/S items, average for P&L, historical for equity) — and what happens when the period-end rate differs from the transaction-date spot. | **FM-3** (insensitive check picks wrong rate, passes silently) + FM-2 (convention) | …the rate-selection boundary (closing vs average vs historical per item type), not just "use the FX rate." |
| **V-E2** | **Partial-period / mid-period entity** | An entity acquired/disposed/onboarded mid-period: only its post-acquisition (or pre-disposal) activity consolidates; opening balances handled specially. | **FM-3** (happy-path check insensitive to the partial-period slice) | …partial-period entities (acquisition/disposal mid-month) and that only the in-scope slice consolidates. |
| **V-E3** | **Late adjustment before sign-off → correction cascade** | An entry arrives after its sub-ledger/posting pass already ran **but before the period is signed off**: it must trigger re-run of the dependent downstream passes (re-post → re-reval → re-eliminate → re-TB) so the still-open close is internally consistent, not a silent patch. **Discriminator: pre-sign-off** — the close is still open, so this is a *correction cascade within the live run*, NOT the post-lock FM-7 reopen channel (V-F3) and NOT a generic ordering-gate (V-F1). | **FM-4** (a late input must re-propagate through dependent passes) | …a *pre-sign-off* late entry and that it forces re-running the downstream passes in dependency order (correction cascade), not a one-off patch. The discriminator is "before lock, while the close is open." |
| **V-E4** | **Rounding residual on translation** | Translating/eliminating at multi-decimal rates leaves sub-unit rounding differences that can make the TB miss zero by pennies; a defined rounding/plug rule is needed. | **FM-3** (a balance check insensitive to a sub-unit residue would false-PASS / false-FAIL) | …rounding differences from FX/translation and a rule that absorbs them (rounding account / plug) so the TB still ties. |
| **V-E5** | **Zero-/negative-balance and reversing accounts** | Contra accounts, credit balances on normally-debit accounts, and zero balances must not be dropped or sign-flipped by posting/reval logic. | **FM-3** (sign/zero edge silently mishandled, passes wrong) | …sign/contra/zero-balance handling (credit balance on a debit account, contra accounts). |
| **V-E6** | **Missing or stale FX rate for a currency** | A rate for some currency/date is unavailable at close time: the design must define a fallback/hold rule rather than posting at zero or skipping. | **FM-3** (insensitive to a missing rate — posts zero, passes) | …the missing-rate boundary and a defined fallback (prior rate / hold the close / flag), not silent zero. |
| **V-E7** | **Net-zero functional balance hiding gross foreign exposure** | A net-zero **functional-currency** balance can still hide **gross** foreign-currency exposure that needs revaluation (offsetting foreign positions net to ~0 in functional terms yet each leg carries unrealized FX). Revaluation must operate on **gross** foreign positions, not the net functional balance. **Distinct from V-E5** (which is contra/sign/zero-balance handling) — this is a gross-vs-net *foreign-exposure* edge, not a sign edge. | **FM-3** (net-balance check insensitive to gross exposure → false-PASS) | …that revaluation must work on gross foreign positions, not net functional balances; a net-zero functional balance does not imply zero FX exposure. Credit "revalue gross FC positions, not net," "net-zero functional can hide gross foreign exposure." **Does not collide with V-E5** (contra/sign) — different boundary. |

### 4.3 Defect-coupling criteria (former Failure-modes — only those with NO `Dep` twin)

Phrased as **concrete coverable claims** (never adjectives). Each names a specific defect coupling AND its
resolution; covered iff the decomposition names the same coupling and a guard. **One-defect-one-place:** a
defect-coupling is kept here ONLY if it has no `Dep`-edge twin. A coupling that shares the *same ordered
pair, same falsifier, and same fix* as an existing `Dep` edge is **not** a separate item — the `Dep` edge
already carries it. The prior F3/F4/F5 (IC residual, accrual double-count, realized/unrealized FX) were
exactly such twins of Dep7/Dep10/Dep11 and have been **removed**; their content is scored on the `Dep` edge.
What remains are the couplings with **no `Dep` twin**.

| # | Name | What it requires (coupling + guard) | FM tag (precludes) | Truth-maker (covered iff named with a guard) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **V-F1** | **Out-of-order pass yields wrong TB** | A downstream pass (reval, elimination, TB) runs on inputs from a pass that hasn't completed (or was re-opened) → wrong result silently propagates. **Twins no single seam** — it generalizes the whole ordering family (Dep1/Dep3/Dep5/Dep6/Dep9), so it is the one canonical *ordering-gate* failure-mode, not a per-edge restatement. Guard: enforce pass-ordering / dependency gate. | **FM-4** (propagation/stale: the global ordering-gate, generalizing the whole ordering family) | …that running passes out of dependency order produces a wrong-but-balanced TB, AND a dependency-ordering gate prevents it. (The *gate as a global mechanism* — distinct from any one Dep-pair's local ordering claim.) |
| **V-F2** | **Balanced-but-wrong (false PASS) TB** | The TB nets to zero yet is materially wrong (e.g. an error and its offsetting error both posted; an account misclassified). "Nets to zero" alone does NOT prove correctness. **No `Dep` twin.** Guard: independent reconciliation / analytic review beyond the balance check. | **FM-3** (false-PASS: a balance check is insensitive to offsetting/misclassification errors) | …that a zero-netting TB can still be wrong, AND a guard beyond the balance check (rec/analytic) is needed. Credit any concrete "balance ≠ correct, need an independent check" statement (e.g. "two offsetting errors net to zero") — **no GFSO vocabulary required**. **Mirrors the GFSO faithfulness residual** — hardest to articulate. |
| **V-F3** | **Silent post-close change (no re-run channel)** | A defect found **after sign-off** has no channel to re-open and re-run the affected passes → the error is invisible/unbooked. **No `Dep` twin** (distinct from V-E3, which is a *pre*-sign-off late entry, and from V-F1, which is mis-ordering of a live run). Guard: a controlled re-open + cascade-rerun + audit-logged adjustment path. | **FM-7** (post-sign-off feedback channel) — *the one runtime mode a static criterion does carry, see §7* | …that **post-sign-off** defects need a controlled re-open & re-run channel (FM-7), not a silent edit. The discriminator is *after lock* + *audit-logged reopen* — not the generic "re-run downstream" shared with V-E3/V-F1. |

> **Note on V-F3 and the operational axis.** V-F3 is the one criterion that names an OPERATIONAL failure mode
> (**FM-7 feedback**, §4.3) at design time — because the *task itself* ("auditable") demands a designed
> reopen channel, so the requirement is a static design predicate even though the mode it guards is a runtime
> one. This does **not** make the static reference responsible for runtime FM-5/FM-6 (see §7) — those have no
> design-time predicate the task statement forces.

## Appendix — settled adjudications carried over (scorer must apply; verbatim from `reference_buckets_v1.md`)

- **V-E4 (rounding residual) vs V-I1 (balance) — SEPARATE, keep both.** V-I1 is the *predicate* (debits=credits /
  nets to zero); V-E4 is a *boundary condition* (sub-unit residue from multi-decimal FX rates) **plus a named
  mechanism** (rounding account / plug) that makes the predicate hold despite it. Different truth-makers: V-I1
  is satisfied by *checking* a sum, V-E4 by *naming the residue source and a plug rule*. **Scorer rule:** an
  agent who folds the plug into the balance check ("we book a rounding line to keep debits=credits") has
  substantively covered V-E4 — **credit V-E4 on meaning**, regardless of which heading they filed it under.
- **N3 (sources assumed valid) vs V-F2 (balanced-but-wrong) — DISTINCT, not a double-count.** N3 is a *declared
  scope-exclusion* (the close does not re-audit each source transaction for fraud/mis-recording — an upstream
  controls function). V-F2 is a *failure-mode within scope* (offsetting/misclassification errors *inside the
  close itself* yield a zero-netting-but-wrong TB; guard = independent rec/analytic). An agent can cover one
  without the other. They share only the slogan "balanced ≠ correct." **Scorer rule:** a single "the books can
  balance and still be wrong" sentence maps to **V-F2 only** — it does not also claim the N3 declarative
  exclusion. No shared coverage credit.
- **D1 granularity — one subtask is correct; credit either granularity.** Sub-ledger close is ONE subtask
  with AP/AR/payroll/… as *sources*, to hold single-meta-level (the inter-ledger interactions that matter —
  control-tie Dep2, IC Dep7 — are already separate `Dep` items). **Scorer rule:** an agent who *splits*
  AP/AR/payroll into multiple components maps them all to D1 — this is the *expected* granularity choice here
  and is **not** a non-redundancy violation; do not penalize the split, and do not require it.
- **Controls / segregation-of-duties — ORTHOGONAL AUTHORITY PLANE, not scored as a decomposition item.**
  SoD (preparer ≠ approver) is a property of the process/authority, not a predicate over the produced TB; it
  is emergent from the Issuer ≠ Executor-on-validation split (`Del` + delegation hierarchy) the GFSO protocol
  already guarantees structurally. **Scorer rule:** do **not** penalize a decomposition for omitting SoD as a
  `D`/`Dep`/`V`/NEGLECTED item, and do **not** award criterion-credit (nor deduct) for an agent who states
  "preparer ≠ approver" — it is a correct statement in a different plane. Force-fitting SoD into a criterion is
  a category error (criterion = artifact predicate; SoD = process/authority predicate). See §3 authority-plane
  section above. This is plane-separation, **not** a NEGLECTED exclusion and **not** a completeness hole.
- **V-E5 (contra/sign/zero) vs V-E7 (gross foreign exposure) — DISTINCT edges, no collision.** V-E5 is
  sign/contra/zero-balance handling (a credit balance on a debit account must not be sign-flipped or dropped);
  V-E7 is the gross-vs-net *foreign-exposure* edge (a net-zero functional balance can hide gross FC positions
  needing revaluation). Different boundary conditions, different fixes. **Scorer rule:** an agent naming only
  "handle contra/credit balances" covers V-E5, not V-E7; "revalue gross FC positions, not net" covers V-E7,
  not V-E5. No shared coverage credit.
